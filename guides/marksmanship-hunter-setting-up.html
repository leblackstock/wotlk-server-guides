Marksmanship core

  • Chimera Shot, Aimed Shot, and Readiness define the cycle.
  • Trueshot Aura covers the physical raid buff when no stronger equivalent is present.
  • Piercing Shots, Wild Quiver, and Improved Steady Shot reward clean weapon uptime.

Fresh-80 support

  • 3/3 Focused Aim removes 3% from the gear hit requirement.
  • Careful Aim converts Intellect into attack power and favors coherent mail upgrades.
  • Reallocate Focused Aim only after the character remains capped in the actual boss set.

Survival branch

  • Improved Tracking rewards selecting the correct creature tracking.
  • Survival Instincts improves core shots and survivability.
  • This baseline avoids modern talents or Classic-era trap-launcher assumptions.

Wolf and Ferocity setup↑ Top

Recommended raid pet

  • Wolf for Furious Howl. Keep it alive, fed, and in range.
  • Use a level-80 Ferocity build centered on Cobra Reflexes, Dash, Bloodthirsty, Spiked Collar, Boar's Speed, Culling the Herd, Spider's Bite, Rabid, and Call of the Wild.
  • Heart of the Phoenix is a strong progression recovery point; move only with a deliberate alternate plan.

Control rules

  • Bind pet attack and pet follow separately. Do not make every ability permanently issue /petattack.
  • Keep Growl off for bosses. Leave Cower off autocast unless pet survival demands it.
  • Use Mend Pet early and pull the pet out of lethal ground effects.
  • Save Call of the Wild for the pull or the raid's chosen burst phase.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Misdirection to focus

Use a friendly focus; fall back to the living pet.

#showtooltip Misdirection /cast [@focus,help,nodead][@pet,exists,nodead] Misdirection

Mouseover Tranquilizing Shot

Tranq a hostile mouseover without dropping the boss target.

#showtooltip Tranquilizing Shot /cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Tranquilizing Shot

Chimera plus free Silencing Shot

Use only when Silencing Shot is not reserved for an interrupt.

#showtooltip Chimera Shot /cast Silencing Shot /cast Chimera Shot

Controlled pet attack

Send the pet deliberately and trigger Kill Command when available.

#showtooltip Kill Command /petattack [harm,nodead] /cast Kill Command

Pet retreat

Pull the pet out immediately and start recovery.

#showtooltip Mend Pet /petfollow /cast Mend Pet

Aspect toggle

Toggle between normal damage and temporary mana recovery.

#showtooltip Aspect of the Viper /castsequence reset=10 Aspect of the Viper, Aspect of the Dragonhawk
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for movement, add, enrage, and target timers.
  • WeakAuras or TellMeWhen for sting, shot cooldowns, Auto Shot, Viper, and pet buffs.
  • Omen for threat before and after Misdirection.

Recommended

  • A swing timer that explicitly supports Auto Shot on the 3.3.5a client.
  • Details/Recount for shot mix, pet uptime, aspect uptime, target swaps, and avoidable deaths.
  • A range display and raid-frame setup that makes Misdirection, Master's Call, and Tranq assignments visible.
