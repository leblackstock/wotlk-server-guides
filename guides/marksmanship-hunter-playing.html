Marks Hunter Playing Guide

Run the original 3.3.5a shot priority, preserve Auto Shot uptime, use Readiness correctly, move cleanly, and control the pet.

Core decision engine↑ Top

Sting

Apply Serpent Sting once, then protect it with timely Chimera Shot refreshes.

Shot priority

Protect Kill Shot, Chimera Shot, and Aimed Shot cooldowns before filling with Steady Shot.

Movement

Move during instant-shot globals and stop long enough for Auto Shot and Steady Shot.

Pet & utility

Keep the wolf alive, control its cooldowns, and use Misdirection, traps, and interrupts as assignments.

Default priority: Kill Shot when usable → keep Serpent Sting active through Chimera Shot → Chimera Shot → Aimed Shot → Arcane Shot in a fresh/low-Armor-Penetration set → Steady Shot. Fire Silencing Shot for damage only when it is not reserved for an interrupt.

Opener

  • Pre-mark with Hunter's Mark if assigned and set the tank as focus.
  • Use Misdirection during the countdown and pre-pot Potion of Speed.
  • Send the pet, apply Serpent Sting, then fire Chimera Shot and Aimed Shot.
  • Use Rapid Fire and racial/on-use cooldowns in the first stable damage window.
  • After the first cooldown cycle, use Readiness to reset key shots and Rapid Fire; do not waste already-ready cooldowns.

Track this

  • Serpent Sting duration and the next Chimera Shot cooldown.
  • Kill Shot, Chimera Shot, Aimed Shot, and Readiness availability.
  • Auto Shot timer so movement ends before the next ranged swing.
  • Aspect of the Viper uptime—leave it immediately when the required mana has been recovered.
  • Pet health, target, range, Furious Howl, and Call of the Wild.

Normal cycle

Single target

Boss

Decision

Protect the Chimera Shot refresh and never delay an available execute for a filler.

Serpent Sting›Chimera Shot›Aimed Shot›Arcane Shot›Steady Shot
  • Kill Shot jumps to the front below 20%.
  • Arcane Shot belongs in a fresh Agility set; a mature high-ArP set may drop it after testing.
  • Steady Shot fills real empty time—it does not outrank a ready signature shot.

Failure to avoid: Refreshing Serpent Sting manually after the opener, delaying Chimera, or hard-casting Steady while a higher-priority shot is ready.

Execution

Movement and Auto Shot

Uptime

Decision

Spend instant-shot globals while moving, then plant before the next ranged swing or Steady cast.

Aimed Shot›Chimera Shot›Disengage›Auto Shot
  • Auto Shot fires while stationary and is not clipped by Steady Shot in Wrath.
  • Use short stutter steps around the swing timer instead of running continuously.
  • Disengage is a positioning tool; verify the landing space before pressing it.

Failure to avoid: Treating every movement mechanic as continuous movement and losing several Auto Shots.

Cooldown reset

Rapid Fire and Readiness

Burst

Decision

Spend the cooldowns Readiness can reset before pressing it, then use the second window where the boss permits full uptime.

Rapid Fire›Chimera Shot›Aimed Shot›Readiness›Rapid Fire
  • Do not press Readiness while Rapid Fire and both main shots are already available.
  • Keep enough fight time after the reset to realize the second haste window.
  • A progression mechanic can be a better Readiness anchor than the pull.

Failure to avoid: Resetting unused cooldowns or activating Rapid Fire immediately before forced movement.

Area damage

Adds and cleave

Multi-target

Decision

Burn priority adds first; use Volley only when several targets will remain stacked through the channel.

Misdirection›Multi-Shot›Volley›Explosive Trap
  • Multi-Shot is quick cleave but must not break crowd control.
  • Volley wins on stable packs, not on targets about to scatter or die.
  • Explosive Trap is feet-placement in baseline 3.3.5a; pre-position only when safe.

Failure to avoid: Channeling Volley while a priority add lives or assuming the later Classic ranged trap launcher exists.

Threat control

Misdirection and survival

Utility

Decision

Move initial or add threat to the assigned tank, then shed personal threat before it becomes an emergency.

Misdirection›Feign Death›Deterrence›Disengage
  • Misdirection is an assignment, not permission to shoot before the tank is ready.
  • Feign Death needs a deliberate cancel/stand-up plan so the hunter resumes damage.
  • Deterrence is a survival cooldown; confirm which raid mechanics it can actually deflect on Hellscream.

Failure to avoid: Misdirecting the wrong unit, feigning after the mob reaches the hunter, or gambling on an untested immunity.

Mana and pet

Sustain the whole kit

Resource

Decision

Recover only the mana needed while keeping the wolf alive and attacking the correct target.

Aspect of the Viper›Mend Pet›Call of the Wild›Aspect of the Dragonhawk
  • Aspect of the Viper is a temporary recovery state with a large damage penalty.
  • Use Mend Pet before lethal pet damage, not after the wolf is already dead.
  • Call of the Wild belongs in the raid's burst plan rather than on an arbitrary trash pull.

Failure to avoid: Leaving Viper active, losing the pet to avoidable damage, or letting the pet attack the wrong add.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Starting a boss set below the real hit cap or carrying large wasted hit above it.
  • Letting Chimera Shot drift and forcing a manual Serpent Sting refresh.
  • Gemming Armor Penetration before the gear has enough native ArP to support it.
  • Moving through Auto Shot windows instead of stutter-stepping around instant shots.
Raid mistakes
  • Leaving Aspect of the Viper active after mana recovery.
  • Treating the pet as passive background damage and losing Furious Howl or Call of the Wild.
  • Macroing Silencing Shot into every attack when the raid expects a real interrupt.
  • Copying WotLK Classic Trap Launcher advice into an original 3.3.5a client.
