◆ The Holy Paladin healing engine↑ Top

Holy Paladin is not a fixed rotation. It is a maintenance-and-decision loop: keep the engine active, predict the next hit, choose the spell that safely covers it, and preserve enough mana for the dangerous part of the encounter.

Direct-heal targetReceives the spell, Glyph of Holy Light splash, and any eligible secondary effect.
→
Beacon targetReceives the eligible direct healing copied by Beacon of Light.

Always maintain

  • Beacon of Light on the assigned target.
  • Sacred Shield on the assigned tank.
  • Judgements of the Pure through a safe Judgement.

Choose the cast

  • Holy Light for dangerous or sustained raid damage.
  • Flash of Light for genuinely low incoming damage.
  • Holy Shock for movement, emergency triage, or an Infusion of Light setup.

Plan the mana

  • Use Divine Plea before desperation.
  • Pair reduced healing with a safe damage window or compensating cooldown.
  • Judge and melee with Seal of Wisdom only when positioning is safe.
Beacon limitation: Beacon copies eligible direct healing from Holy Light, Flash of Light, and Holy Shock. Glyph of Holy Light splash and the Flash of Light HoT do not transfer.

Normal assignment

Routine tank damage

Baseline

Keep active

Beacon, Sacred Shield, Judgements of the Pure, assigned aura, and Seal of Wisdom.

Normal response

Maintain buffs›Holy Light›Beacon transfer
  • Pre-cast Holy Light when a predictable swing or special is due.
  • Cancel only when the cast is clearly unnecessary and mana is actually constrained.
  • Use Flash of Light during low damage rather than pretending every second is heroic Lich King.

Failure to avoid: Refreshing Beacon or Sacred Shield after they expire during the burst instead of before it.

Split assignment

Two tanks taking damage

Beacon

Target plan

Beacon one tank and cast directly on the other. Reverse only when the damage pattern or range demands it.

Normal response

Beacon Tank A›Heal Tank B
  • Direct-heal the tank taking the less predictable or more urgent damage.
  • Use the Beacon target as your safety net, not as a reason to ignore range.
  • Do not swap Beacon casually; it costs mana and a global cooldown.

Failure to avoid: Casting repeatedly into your Beacon while the second tank receives no copied healing.

Tank buster

Sudden tank spike

Emergency

Immediate response

Holy Shock›Infusion cast›Divine Favor
  • Use Divine Favor before the important heal when a guaranteed crit matters.
  • Use Hand of Sacrifice as a planned external, preferably with Divine Protection or Divine Shield protecting you.
  • Lay on Hands is a real emergency tool. Saving it for next expansion produces zero healing.

Failure to avoid: Stacking every cooldown after the tank is already dead.

Group damage

Heavy raid-wide damage

Raid support

Normal response

  • Keep the assigned tank safe first.
  • Cast Holy Light into injured clustered players to exploit Glyph of Holy Light splash.
  • Use Aura Mastery with the resistance aura matching the incoming school, or Concentration Aura for silence/pushback problems.
  • Use Divine Guardian before the damage lands, then cancel Divine Sacrifice or protect yourself as Hellscream scripting requires.

Failure to avoid: Chasing raid bars while the assigned tank falls through the floorboards.

Repositioning

Movement phase

Mobile

Before moving

  • Refresh Beacon, Sacred Shield, and Judgements of the Pure if they will expire.
  • Pre-cast a heal to land as movement begins.

While moving

Holy Shock›Instant Flash of Light›Hands / Aura Mastery

Failure to avoid: Finishing one heroic Holy Light while standing in a mechanic that deletes the healer.

Resource plan

Mana trouble

Sustain
ComfortableUse Holy Light as required and keep full maintenance.
Plan nowFind the next Divine Plea and potion window.
CriticalThrottle casts, call for help, and stop wasting globals.
  • Use Divine Plea during low damage, phase transitions, immunity windows, or with compensating cooldowns.
  • A mana potion is usually stronger when used early enough that it prevents panic later.
  • Seal of Wisdom contact is powerful, but dead healers regenerate poorly.

Failure to avoid: Waiting until empty mana to begin thinking about mana.
