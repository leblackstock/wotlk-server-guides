Use the fully allocated Bear build for mitigation, threat, Rage, recovery, and dungeon control. Document exact debuff-coverage swaps before adopting a raid-composition variant.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Tank foundation

  • Survival of the Fittest supplies the full 6% reduction to chance to be critically hit, so the Bear does not chase 540 Defense.
  • Thick Hide and Protector of the Pack support physical survival.
  • Natural Reaction adds dodge, Rage from dodges, and damage reduction in Bear Form.

Threat and control

  • Mangle anchors single-target threat and bleed support.
  • Berserk enables a planned multi-target Mangle window and breaks fear on use.
  • Primal Precision supplies 10 expertise and refunds missed finisher Energy for the shared Feral tree, though Bear values its expertise directly.

Known flex boundary

  • The mono-Bear baseline keeps core survival and threat complete.
  • Enhanced Demoralizing Roar requires explicit later talent swaps; never claim the baseline supplies the enhanced effect.
  • Any raid variant must preserve 71 points and document exactly which survival or threat tool moved.

Debuff truth and raid variants↑ Top

What the baseline supplies

  • Faerie Fire (Feral) armor reduction when assigned.
  • Baseline Demoralizing Roar attack-power reduction.
  • Mangle's bleed-damage support when no equivalent effect is present.

What requires a declared swap

  • Improved Demoralizing Roar is not silently included.
  • Infected Wounds or other control variants change points and assignments.
  • Document the exact points removed and verify all 71 remain allocated.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Mouseover Growl

Taunt a hostile mouseover without losing the main target; fall back to the current target.

#showtooltip Growl /cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Growl

Focus Bash

Stun the hostile focus without changing the tank target; fall back normally.

#showtooltip Bash /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Bash

Direct Bear charge

Use the Bear charge on the current hostile target without adding an unsafe target change.

#showtooltip Feral Charge - Bear /cast Feral Charge - Bear

Emergency survival

Activate temporary health; Barkskin and Frenzied Regeneration remain separate timing decisions.

#showtooltip Survival Instincts /cast Survival Instincts
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for burst, swap, movement, and add timing.
  • Threat plates that show threat state on every visible enemy, not only the selected target.
  • A clear boss frame and debuff display for tank-swap stacks and survival timers.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Savage Defense, Lacerate, Demoralizing Roar, and survival cooldowns.
  • Details/Recount to review deaths, damage taken, missed taunts, interrupts, and threat losses rather than damage rank.
  • A healer-mana and external-cooldown display for pull pacing and planned burst coverage.
