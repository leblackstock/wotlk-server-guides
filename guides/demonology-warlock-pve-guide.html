Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard original-client WotLK 3.3.5a behavior is the baseline. Verify Demonic Pact overwrites, pet inheritance and pathing, Metamorphosis handling, and Immolation Aura range on Hellscream.

Before the pull

  • Use Fel Armor, summon the Felguard, create a Healthstone, and place a Soulstone on the agreed target.
  • Bind pet attack, follow, passive, Cleave, and Intercept rather than trusting every automatic decision.
  • Confirm the assigned curse; use Curse of the Elements when the group lacks the equivalent spell-damage debuff.
  • Know the first Metamorphosis window and whether approaching the target is safe or unnecessary.

First fresh-80 moves

  • Train every level-80 rank and install the complete 0/56/15 baseline and glyph set.
  • Enter normal and heroic dungeons immediately; practice keeping the Felguard alive and on the selected target.
  • Replace leveling gear and work toward the level-83 hit target without treating it as a dungeon-entry gate.
  • Learn the three filler states before attempting close-range Metamorphosis optimization.
