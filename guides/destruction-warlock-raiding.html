Destro Lock Raid DPS Guide

Handle short cooldowns, target swaps, Imp safety, movement, control, and encounter responsibilities by mode.

PreparationAssignmentsEncountersSources
Where this chapter fits: This chapter is later progression, not the starting expectation for a new level 80. Complete the self-contained setup, practice the priority and pet controls in normal and heroic dungeons, and enter raids only after the character and player are ready.
Raid scope: these are Destruction Warlock responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of the Frost Wyrm for planned raid progression; it is not required for ordinary dungeon entry.
  • Firecracker Salmon or Fish Feast for a practical spell-power food option.
  • Potion of Speed for a planned haste window; Potion of Wild Magic is a critical-strike/spell-power alternative whose timing depends on the specialization.
  • Soul Shards, a Healthstone, a Soulstone, repaired gear, and the correct pet before the pull.

Confirm before pull

  • Which curse or debuff the Warlock is responsible for and who covers it after a death or target swap.
  • Primary target, add priority, crowd-control assignment, and whether the pet stays on the boss or swaps.
  • Demonic Circle location and the movement mechanic it is intended to solve.
  • Imp Firebolt control, short-cooldown timing, and Shadowfury assignments.
  • Soulstone target, Healthstone availability, and Soulshatter timing.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Maintain the assigned curse and fire priority while following target order. Use Shadowfury for assigned add control, and on Anub'arak choose Seed of Corruption or Rain of Fire only when stacked adds justify AoE.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep Immolate on the boss, switch immediately to Bone Spikes with direct burst, and use instant globals while relocating during Bone Storm. Recall the Imp before Coldflame or unsafe pathing removes it.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Use burst on the called add, reserve Shadowfury for assigned control, and keep the Imp on the correct physical-or-magic target. Move early from Death and Decay rather than finishing Incinerate.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep the boss priority active and use immediate direct damage on Blood Beasts only when assigned. Shadowfury can support control if the raid plans for it; do not send the Imp or apply slow setup to beasts indiscriminately.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Switch hard to Volatile Ooze and Gas Cloud, use short cooldowns for real burst, and move before Malleable Goo. Pre-place Demonic Circle: Summon and protect the Imp from bad paths and slime.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

On Princes, damage only the empowered target and use direct spells on Kinetic Bombs when assigned. On Blood-Queen, follow bite order, maintain the fire priority through planned movement, and preserve the Imp during the air phase.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Prioritize Blazing Skeletons and Suppressors, use Shadowfury for planned control, and AoE only stable stacks. The Imp stays on the priority target rather than padding a harmless add.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Manage Unchained Magic and Instability; stop casting before stacks become lethal. Recall the Imp for unsafe air phases, break only assigned tombs, and use tomb line-of-sight to reset Mystic Buffet.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAll rolesNeeds Hellscream test

Maintain boss Immolate, burst Val'kyr and Raging Spirits, and preserve Shadowfury for an assigned control moment. Verify Hellscream Imp pathing, Empowered Imp, and Vile Spirit behavior before relying on a specialized pet or stun route.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Stay in the assigned realm, pre-place Demonic Circle around cutter movement, and move early with combustion or consumption. Keep the Imp on the correct realm target and resume the short-cooldown priority only from a safe plant position.
