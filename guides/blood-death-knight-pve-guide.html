WotLK 3.3.5 Blood DK Tank Quick Start

You already know how to tank a raid. Use these pages to move a geared Death Knight into the 43/23/5 personal-survival build for Heroic Lich King 25 progression without the ICC zone-wide buff.

Your first transition check is Frost Presence. Blood names the talent tree; Frost Presence remains the tank stance in patch 3.3.5. Put the presence check into your ready-check routine after a respec, death, or vehicle transition.

Why 43/23/5 wins here

  • Will of the Necropolis reduces any damage that would take you below 35% health—and damage taken while at or below 35%—by 15% in 3.3.5.
  • Anticipation adds 5% dodge; Frigid Dreadplate makes melee attacks 3% more likely to miss.
  • Improved Death Strike, Improved Rune Tap, and Vampiric Blood create the strongest personal recovery package in the DK trees.

When it is not the answer

  • Use 43/26/2 only when the 25-player roster truly lacks the 20% melee-haste raid buff. That is a raid-composition tax, not better personal survival.
  • Use a Morbidity/AoE off-spec for fast dungeons or dedicated trash if needed. It gives up boss durability.
  • Do not trade mitigation talents for parsing talents on no-buff LK progression; threat from Icy Touch is already exceptional.

What changes in your combat engine

Keep your existing encounter awareness, but stop treating lit runes and Runic Power as automatically spendable. In this build, the correct button now is the one that leaves the required response available for the next boss event.

1. Runes

Keep one Frost + one Unholy rune available for Death Strike. Spend Blood runes to activate Blade Barrier, but reserve a Blood rune for Rune Tap or Vampiric Blood when their window is close.

2. Diseases

Maintain Frost Fever and Blood Plague. In 3.3.5, Death Strike heals 5% maximum health per disease—not from recent damage taken.

3. Runic Power

Icebound Fortitude and Anti-Magic Shell each cost 20 RP. Keep that reserve when either may be needed; queue Rune Strike only from surplus.

4. Emergency life

Raise Dead into Death Pact is a real heal. Army of the Dead is assignment-only: uncontrolled ghoul taunts can ruin LK or add positioning.

Maintain

  1. Frost Presence.
  2. 540 Defense skill.
  3. Blade Barrier during active tanking.
  4. Two diseases before a planned Death Strike.

Plan

  1. One personal cooldown for each assigned Soul Reaper.
  2. One named external if the personal is weak or unavailable.
  3. A taunt handoff before the five-second Shadow detonation.
  4. A recovery button after the burst, not all buttons before it.

Never improvise

  1. Do not spend the last 20 RP before danger.
  2. Do not let both Death Strike runes disappear into threat.
  3. Do not stack every cooldown into one Reaper.
  4. Do not use pets or Army without the raid lead’s plan.

Your first pulls in this spec

Use farm content to rehearse the resource and cooldown rules below before you ask the build to carry a progression Soul Reaper.

Boss pull and threat lead

Icy Touch → Plague Strike → Death Strike → Blood Strike → Pestilence. Icy Touch in Frost Presence is the snap-threat anchor. Queue Rune Strike after a dodge/parry only when the 20-RP defensive reserve remains intact; do not burn Empower Rune Weapon on a routine pull.

Multiple-target pickup

Death and Decay, if its three runes are safe → Icy Touch/Plague Strike → Pestilence → Blood Boil. Tab Icy Touch and Rune Strike into the dangerous target. Dark Command is the primary taunt; Death Grip is the backup taunt or reposition only when the target can move safely.

Planned boss burst

Bank 20 RP and the required runes → use the assigned personal before impact → complete the taunt handoff → recover with Death Strike or Rune Tap. Use Vampiric Blood early enough for healers to exploit it. Chain one planned external; do not stack every wall on the same Soul Reaper.

Emergency and control

Vampiric Blood → Rune Tap → Death Strike → Death Pact, adding a Healthstone or Empower Rune Weapon when needed. Mind Freeze is the free melee interrupt in this build; Strangulate is the ranged silence. Call a failed taunt or unavailable interrupt immediately.

You are not progression-ready in this spec until the plan is executable. A 43/23/5 tree cannot rescue an unplanned Soul Reaper, a sub-540-Defense role set, a missed taunt call, or resources spent immediately before the burst.

Heroic LK25 pull checklist

Player: verify your kit

  • 43/23/5 tree and correct major glyphs.
  • Frost Presence active; slow 2H equipped; Stoneskin Gargoyle runeforge present.
  • 540+ Defense skill after all swaps.
  • Flask of Stoneblood, stamina food, Indestructible Potion, Healthstone, and Heavy Frostweave Bandage ready.
  • Rune, Runic Power, diseases, boss swing, Soul Reaper, external cooldowns, and taunt target visible.

Raid leader: lock the assignment

  • Name the player’s exact role: LK tank, Shambling Horror tank, Raging Spirit tank, Soul Reaper swap, Vile Spirit soaker, or Frostmourne return tank.
  • State the taunt order and who owns the delayed hit versus the hasted-melee window for each Soul Reaper.
  • Assign a healer or external to every gap in the player’s personal-cooldown sheet.
  • Call the first Defile movement direction and transition-edge position before the pull.
  • State whether pets or Army are permitted for this attempt.
Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard WotLK 3.3.5 behavior is the baseline and the Icecrown zone buff is assumed disabled. Where the raid page says Verify on Hellscream, the player should report the observed behavior and the raid leader should update the pull sheet before treating it as settled.
