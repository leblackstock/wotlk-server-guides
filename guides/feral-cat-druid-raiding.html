Feral Cat Raid DPS Guide

Handle positional uptime, bleeds, target swaps, interrupts, battle resurrection, and encounter responsibilities by mode.

Where this chapter fits: This chapter is later progression, not the starting expectation for a new level 80. Complete the self-contained setup, practice the role in normal and heroic dungeons, and enter raids only after the character and player are ready.
Raid scope: these are Feral Cat Druid responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of Endless Rage for the default attack-power flask.
  • Fish Feast or equivalent Agility food after verifying the available feast.
  • Potion of Speed for a planned full-uptime window.

Confirm before pull

  • Confirm Mangle or equivalent bleed-debuff coverage, Rebirth, Innervate, and emergency-Bear expectations.
  • Identify rear access, cleave sides, charge paths, and the first forced movement.
  • State target-swap and stun priorities before spending combo points.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Maintain the Cat engine on durable targets and match bleed setup to each target's remaining life. On Anub'arak, wait for tank threat before Swipe (Cat) and respect burrower control.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Attack from a safe rear quarter, switch immediately to Bone Spikes, and use Dash or Feral Charge - Cat only through a safe path during Bone Storm.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Follow add priority and crowd control, use Maim only when assigned, and avoid building full bleeds on targets that will die before their ticks matter.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Remain on the boss unless assigned to Blood Beasts. Never chase a beast through another player's path; immediate Mangle damage and control matter more than a full bleed setup.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Switch hard to Volatile Ooze and Gas Cloud, move before Malleable Goo, and choose builders according to rear access and target life. Use Barkskin for predictable raid damage.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

On Princes, attack only the empowered target and preserve position through swaps. On Blood-Queen, follow bite order, move early for Swarming Shadows, and protect the next Roar or Rip deadline.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Prioritize Blazing Skeletons and Suppressors, use Swipe only on secured packs, and reserve Maim for an assigned eligible cast. This encounter rewards correct target selection over long boss-style setup.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Respect Chilled to the Bone stacks and movement, stop attacking when required, and break only assigned Ice Tombs. Plan Roar duration around air phases and phase-three resets.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utilityNeeds Hellscream test

Burn Val'kyr and Raging Spirits by priority, move early for Defile, and do not compromise positioning to preserve a bleed. Verify Hellscream charge and target behavior before depending on a specialized route.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Stay in the assigned realm, use Barkskin before predictable pulses, and move early around cutters. Keep finishers aligned with periods of actual target access rather than the pull timer alone.
