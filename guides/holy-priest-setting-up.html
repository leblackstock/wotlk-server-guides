18/53/0 standard fresh-80 Holy raid build

This version keeps the crucial Discipline mana tools and the full Holy raid-healing engine. Move flex points only when raid composition and actual logs show a reason.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Core engine

  • Serendipity converts spot healing into fast group or large single-target healing.
  • Circle of Healing, Divine Providence, and Prayer of Mending form the mobile raid-healing core.
  • Guardian Spirit is the defining external cooldown.

Mana foundation

  • Meditation allows Spirit regeneration while casting.
  • Inner Focus removes the mana cost of one expensive spell.
  • Mental Agility reduces the cost of the instant spells used constantly.

Real flex points

  • Body and Soul is useful only when coordinated with any Discipline Priest.
  • Lightwell is valuable when the raid will click it; otherwise the point is wasted.
  • Empowered Renew and Test of Faith shift value with spell usage and progression damage.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Mouseover Flash Heal

Heal the mouseover without losing the hostile or assigned target.

#showtooltip Flash Heal /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Flash Heal

Mouseover Prayer of Mending

Start or redirect the bounce quickly.

#showtooltip Prayer of Mending /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Prayer of Mending

Guardian Spirit priority

Mouseover first, then focus, then current friendly target.

#showtooltip Guardian Spirit /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][@focus,help,nodead][help,nodead] Guardian Spirit

Self-or-mouseover Dispel Magic

Dispel a friendly mouseover; hold Alt to guarantee self.

#showtooltip Dispel Magic /cast [mod:alt,@player][@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead] Dispel Magic
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for predictable damage and movement timers.
  • HealBot or equivalent raid frames for range, debuffs, aggro, and click-casting.
  • Decursive for fast magic and disease assignment visibility.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Serendipity, Surge of Light, Prayer of Mending charges, and mana cooldowns.
  • Details/Recount to review deaths, overhealing, spell mix, and cooldown use—not to race another healer.
  • Clique only if it is confirmed compatible with the chosen raid frames and 3.3.5a client.
