Boss hit cap
8% / 263 rating
Subtract 1% for each point in Focused Aim; 3/3 leaves 5% from gear, or 164 rating.
Fresh-80 rule
Agility first
Cap hit, then build a coherent Agility set. Do not gem Armor Penetration because an endgame list does.
Pet
Wolf • Ferocity
Furious Howl and Call of the Wild add real raid damage; a dead or passive pet does not.
Core glyphs
Glyph of Serpent Sting • Glyph of Steady Shot
Glyph of Chimera Shot is the usual third major when latency permits the shorter cycle.
Combat engine
Sting → priority shots → movement weave → pet
Sting

Apply Serpent Sting once, then protect it with timely Chimera Shot refreshes.

Shot priority

Protect Kill Shot, Chimera Shot, and Aimed Shot cooldowns before filling with Steady Shot.

Movement

Move during instant-shot globals and stop long enough for Auto Shot and Steady Shot.

Pet & utility

Keep the wolf alive, control its cooldowns, and use Misdirection, traps, and interrupts as assignments.

Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard original-client WotLK 3.3.5a behavior is the baseline. The later WotLK Classic Trap Launcher: Explosive Trap spell is not assumed; traps are placed at the hunter's feet unless Hellscream explicitly adds a custom launcher.

Before the pull

  • Aspect of the Dragonhawk active; switch to Aspect of the Viper only when mana requires it.
  • Trueshot Aura covered, ammo stocked, weapon durability checked, and the correct tracking type enabled.
  • Wolf summoned, fed, talented, healed, and on the intended passive/defensive setting.
  • Hunter's Mark ownership and Misdirection target agreed before the countdown.
  • Hit cap checked against a level-83 target after talents, racial party aura, food, and gear.

First fresh-80 moves

  • Buy or farm a reliable ranged weapon; weapon DPS dominates small secondary-stat debates.
  • Reach the correct hit cap without wasting rating above it.
  • Learn Serpent Sting → Chimera Shot refresh timing on a target dummy.
  • Bind pet attack, pet follow, Misdirection, Feign Death, and Tranquilizing Shot.
  • Practice moving during instant shots and stopping long enough for Auto Shot and Steady Shot.
