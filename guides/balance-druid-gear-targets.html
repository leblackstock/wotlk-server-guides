Emblems and tier↑ Top

Heroism and Triumph

  • Use Ward of the Violet Citadel or Sundial of the Exiled when those slots are still weak.
  • Use Triumph catch-up pieces to repair the largest armor gaps.
  • Do not save every emblem while wearing leveling gear.

Tier progression

  • Build T9 as an accessible bridge, then compare Balance T10 bonuses against actual offset pieces.
  • Keep hit coherent while changing tier pieces.
  • A set bonus does not replace missing gems, enchants, or movement discipline.

Frost

  • Buy the largest reliable tier or off-set upgrade first.
  • Confirm that a purchase does not create an unplanned hit deficit.
  • Retain useful hit and haste swap pieces.
