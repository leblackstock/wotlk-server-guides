Understanding the 676 haste number
  • It assumes 15% Judgements of the Pure, 5% spell haste from Wrath of Air Totem, and 3% haste from Swift Retribution or Improved Moonkin Form.
  • At roughly 676 haste rating under those assumptions, the global cooldown and base 1.5-second casts reach the one-second floor.
  • Holy Light continues to become faster beyond that point, so 676 is not a universal hard cap.
  • Missing raid buffs changes the rating required. Judge the raid you actually have, not the raid composition living in a spreadsheet castle.

◆ Level-80 gearing path↑ Top

Fresh level 80

  • Equip healing gear with Intellect, spell power, haste, MP5, or crit.
  • Buy Libram of Renewal immediately when available.
  • Farm Soul Preserver from normal Culling of Stratholme.
  • Use reputation, normal/heroic dungeons, crafted gear, and cheap BoEs to replace low-item-level slots.

Raid-ready base set

  • Build enough mana to sustain the expected Holy Light workload.
  • Favor haste on most gear slots without sacrificing large Intellect upgrades.
  • Carry a mana trinket pair and a throughput alternative.
  • Use a one-handed healing weapon and shield; off-hands have lower budget and heavier competition.

Encounter-tuned sets

  • General Intellect/haste set for progression.
  • Extra-sustain setup for long or spread encounters.
  • Throughput setup for short, violent healing checks.
  • Valithria set that values raw healing because encounter mechanics refill mana.
