Core job
Prevent the next death
Holy is a flexible raid healer. Choose by damage pattern, not by a rigid rotation.
Default rhythm
PoM → CoH → triage
Keep Prayer of Mending moving, use Circle of Healing on real clusters, then fill intelligently.
Fresh-80 rule
Mana first, speed second
A healer with empty mana has zero throughput. Add haste after sustained activity is comfortable.
Combat engine
Predict → choose → land → recover
Triage

Use the smallest spell that lands safely before the next damage.

Serendipity

Build with Flash Heal or Binding Heal; spend on Prayer of Healing or Greater Heal.

Prayer

Aim Prayer of Mending and Circle of Healing where several players will take damage.

Mana

Plan Inner Focus, Shadowfiend, Hymn of Hope, and lower-cost fillers early.

Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard original-client WotLK 3.3.5a behavior is the baseline. Hellscream may alter sources, tuning, cooldown resets, or encounter scripting; uncertain interactions are labeled for live testing.

Before the pull

  • Use Inner Fire and the assigned raid prayers; carry candles for group buffs.
  • Confirm raid-healing, tank-support, dispel, and Guardian Spirit assignments.
  • Pre-place Lightwell only where the raid can actually click it safely.
  • Cast Prayer of Mending on the pull target and pre-Renew active tanks when damage is imminent.

First fresh-80 moves

  • Train every rank-80 heal and install raid frames that show range, aggro, diseases, and dispellable magic.
  • Buy or farm a mana trinket; enchant usable gear instead of waiting for a fantasy best-in-slot set.
  • Run normal Trial of the Champion, heroic dungeons, daily randoms, and weekly raids for Triumph and Frost emblems.
  • Enter raids once the set is coherent and enchanted; execution beats another week of shopping.
