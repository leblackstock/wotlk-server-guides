WotLK 3.3.5 Playing your Shadow Priest

Execute the Shadow priority, preserve DoT value, channel Mind Flay cleanly, move without going idle, and recover mana.

Combat enginePlaybooksCooldownsMistakesSources

Core decision engine↑ Top

DoTs

Maintain Vampiric Touch and Devouring Plague without clipping their final ticks.

Shadow Weaving

Reach five stacks before the lasting Shadow Word: Pain application.

Channel

Fit complete Mind Flay ticks between higher-priority events.

Mana & support

Use Shadowfiend, Dispersion, Replenishment, and Hymn of Hope deliberately.

Default priority: Vampiric Touch and Devouring Plague maintained → Mind Blast on cooldown when Replenishment or damage value justifies it → Shadow Word: Death when movement or execute timing favors it → Mind Flay as the channel filler. Apply Shadow Word: Pain only after five Shadow Weaving stacks.

Opener

  • Vampiric Touch → Devouring Plague → Mind Blast → Mind Flay until five Shadow Weaving stacks.
  • Apply Shadow Word: Pain at five stacks, ideally during the intended opening proc window.
  • Continue the priority; Pain and Suffering refreshes Shadow Word: Pain through Mind Flay.
  • Do not delay required movement or mechanics to force a prettier opener.

Track this

  • Vampiric Touch and Devouring Plague remaining duration.
  • Shadow Word: Pain and the five-stack Shadow Weaving condition used for its first cast.
  • Mind Blast cooldown and Replenishment need.
  • Shadowfiend, Dispersion, Hymn of Hope, and mana percentage.
  • The current Mind Flay tick so a channel is not broken accidentally.

Opening sequence

Build five stacks first

Snapshot

Decision

The durable Shadow Word: Pain application waits until Shadow Weaving reaches five stacks.

Vampiric Touch›Devouring Plague›Mind Blast›Mind Flay›Shadow Word: Pain
  • Pain and Suffering updates spell power on refresh but preserves important initial-state effects including the Shadow Weaving state.
  • Do not rebuild the opener after every short mechanic; only reapply when Pain actually falls.

Failure to avoid: casting Shadow Word: Pain at four stacks and carrying an 8% Shadow Weaving bonus through the entire refresh chain.

Single target

Protect DoT ticks

Priority

Decision

Refresh Vampiric Touch and Devouring Plague after their final tick, not several seconds early.

Vampiric Touch›Devouring Plague›Mind Blast›Mind Flay
  • Begin the cast early enough for the new Vampiric Touch to land after expiration.
  • Use Mind Flay as elastic filler; preserve higher-priority cooldowns.

Failure to avoid: clipping repeated final ticks because the timer tracks cast start instead of aura expiration.

Channel control

Count Mind Flay ticks

Execution

Decision

Choose one, two, or three ticks based on the next DoT or cooldown, then break only after the intended tick lands.

Mind Flay›Mind Blast›Vampiric Touch
  • Latency and the 3.3.5 client change how close the next cast can be queued.
  • A visible tick bar is more reliable than guessing from the animation.

Failure to avoid: mashing the next spell and repeatedly losing a tick without gaining a faster high-priority cast.

Multiple targets

Multi-DoT with a death clock

Adds

Decision

DoT only targets that will live long enough; direct damage wins on short-lived priority adds.

Vampiric Touch›Devouring Plague›Mind Sear
  • Vampiric Touch on several durable targets can be excellent.
  • Devouring Plague is limited to one target; put it where the full duration matters.
  • Mind Sear needs enough nearby targets and a safe channel.

Failure to avoid: spending three globals on an add that dies before the second tick.

Movement

Pre-position and spend instants

Mobile

Decision

Move during global cooldowns, refresh only legitimately due DoTs, and use Shadow Word: Death when its recoil is safe.

Devouring Plague›Shadow Word: Death›Power Word: Shield
  • Do not clip a healthy DoT simply to have something to press.
  • Fade removes movement-impairing effects only with Improved Shadowform.

Failure to avoid: standing still for one more Mind Flay tick and dropping the encounter mechanic on the raid.

Survival and mana

Use Dispersion deliberately

Soak

Decision

Dispersion is a 90% damage-reduction and mana tool, not immunity; schedule it where lost casts buy survival or assignment value.

Shadowfiend›Dispersion›Hymn of Hope
  • Use Shadowfiend on a target it can reach and survive.
  • Dispersion can cover movement or a planned soak while restoring mana.
  • Hymn of Hope needs a safe channel and raid awareness.

Failure to avoid: treating Dispersion as immunity and discovering the mechanic still applies, knocks back, or kills through its remaining damage.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Applying Shadow Word: Pain before five Shadow Weaving stacks.
  • Clipping Vampiric Touch or Devouring Plague before the last tick.
  • Treating Mind Flay like a three-tick prison instead of an elastic filler.
Raid mistakes
  • Ignoring the 17% boss spell-hit requirement and blaming random misses on scripting.
  • Multi-DoTing targets too short-lived to repay the globals.
  • Using Dispersion as if it grants immunity or saving Shadowfiend until the boss becomes untargetable.
