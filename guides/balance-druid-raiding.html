Balance Druid Raid DPS Guide

Handle Eclipse windows, movement, target swaps, utility assignments, and encounter responsibilities by mode.

Where this chapter fits: This chapter is later progression, not the starting expectation for a new level 80. Complete the self-contained setup, practice the role in normal and heroic dungeons, and enter raids only after the character and player are ready.
Raid scope: these are Balance Druid responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of the Frost Wyrm for the default spell-power flask.
  • Fish Feast or equivalent spell-power food after verifying the available feast.
  • Potion of Speed for a planned casting window; mana potion when completion is otherwise at risk.

Confirm before pull

  • Confirm Faerie Fire, Innervate, Rebirth, and control assignments.
  • Inspect Starfall range, treant pathing, and the first movement timer.
  • State the gear hit assumption rather than silently counting a raid debuff.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Maintain useful boss effects across target changes and reserve Typhoon for an agreed control job. On Anub'arak, use Starfall only when its radius cannot disrupt burrower control or scarabs.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep Eclipse moving, switch immediately to Bone Spikes, and use genuinely due instant globals during Bone Storm. Check Starfall against nearby trash before the pull.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Follow add priority, use Remove Curse when assigned, and do not pad durable DoTs across controlled or low-priority targets. Preserve Typhoon for the raid's positioning plan.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep damage on the boss unless assigned to Blood Beasts. Use roots or Typhoon only under the control plan; uncontrolled knockback can send a beast toward another player.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Switch hard to Volatile Ooze and Gas Cloud, move before Malleable Goo, and cast DoTs only when the add will live for useful ticks. Save instant globals for the planned route.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

On Princes, attack only the empowered target and help with Kinetic Bombs only when assigned. On Blood-Queen, protect Eclipse uptime by moving early and follow the bite order.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Prioritize Blazing Skeletons and Suppressors, control with roots or Typhoon only by assignment, and use Hurricane on stable packs that the tanks have secured.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Respect Unchained Magic and Instability, stop casting before stacks become lethal, and break only assigned Ice Tombs. Use line-of-sight correctly for Mystic Buffet resets.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utilityNeeds Hellscream test

Burn Val'kyr and Raging Spirits by priority, use movement globals during Defile routes, and never let Starfall replace the raid's Vile Spirit control plan. Verify Hellscream-specific targeting before relying on it.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Remain in the assigned realm, move early for combustion or consumption, and keep Eclipse casts aligned around cutter movement. Typhoon and Starfall remain control-sensitive tools.
