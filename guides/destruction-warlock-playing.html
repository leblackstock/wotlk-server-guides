Destro Lock Playing Guide

Core decision engine↑ Top

Immolate

Keep Immolate active so Conflagrate remains available without consuming it when glyphed.

Bolts

Cast Chaos Bolt on useful cooldowns and fill with Incinerate while the required effects are active.

Backdraft

Spend the short cast-speed window on useful fire casts and move or tap before mana becomes a crisis.

Imp

Keep the Imp safe and active, and queue Firebolt deliberately when manual control improves uptime.

Default priority: Fel Armor and Glyph of Life Tap buff → assigned curse → Immolate → Conflagrate → Chaos Bolt → Incinerate filler → refresh effects only when needed.

Opener

  • Use a safe pre-pull Life Tap when Glyph of Life Tap is equipped; start with Fel Armor and the Imp ready.
  • Precast Immolate only when the tank and pull timer allow it; otherwise establish the assigned curse first.
  • Use Conflagrate once Immolate is active, then Chaos Bolt.
  • Fill with Incinerate and keep the Imp casting Firebolt on the approved target.

Track this

  • Immolate, Conflagrate, Chaos Bolt, Backdraft, and the assigned curse.
  • Glyph of Life Tap, mana, health, threat, and Soulshatter.
  • Imp target, health, range, Firebolt, Empowered Imp, and Blood Pact.
  • Shadowfury assignment, target life, and whether a new Immolate can deliver meaningful value.

Durable target

Run the fire priority

Default

Decision

Maintain Immolate, use the short cooldowns, then fill with Incinerate.

Immolate›Conflagrate›Chaos Bolt›Incinerate
  • Conflagrate requires Immolate and does not consume it with Glyph of Conflagrate.
  • Cooldowns remain subordinate to an urgent movement or control assignment.

Failure to avoid: casting Incinerate into a target without Immolate or delaying short cooldowns through several fillers.

Backdraft active

Spend fast casts well

Proc

Decision

Use Backdraft charges on useful Destruction casts rather than movement, an unnecessary refresh, or downtime.

Conflagrate›Backdraft›Chaos Bolt›Incinerate
  • Plan movement before Conflagrate when the next globals would otherwise be lost.
  • Do not hold Conflagrate indefinitely for a perfect Backdraft sequence.

Failure to avoid: triggering Backdraft immediately before a mechanic that prevents casting.

Urgent target

Burst before setup expires

Swap

Decision

Use immediate useful damage and add Immolate only when the target will live long enough for Conflagrate and ticks.

Shadowfury›Chaos Bolt›Conflagrate
  • Shadowfury can create control time before damage when the target is stunnable.
  • Do not recast a long curse on an add that will die in seconds.

Failure to avoid: spending the entire add lifetime preparing a full boss opener.

Forced movement

Move without panic

Mobile

Decision

Tap safely, use an available instant, command the Imp, or return through Demonic Circle.

Life Tap›Conflagrate›Demonic Circle: Teleport
  • Do not force Conflagrate if Immolate is absent merely to have an instant cast.
  • Use Demonic Circle only after placing it for a real mechanic.

Failure to avoid: Life Tapping into lethal damage or refreshing Immolate far too early during every movement.

Stacked pack

Control before AoE

AoE

Decision

Stun when assigned, then use Seed or Rain of Fire according to pack life and movement.

Shadowfury›Seed of Corruption›Rain of Fire
  • Seed durable stacks; use Rain of Fire for short stable packs.
  • Respect crowd control and tank threat before opening.

Failure to avoid: breaking control or stunning before the tank can position the pack.

Pet hazard

Keep the Imp casting

Pet

Decision

Recall before avoidable damage or transitions, then resend when a safe target is available.

Firebolt›Empowered Imp›Blood Pact
  • Manual Firebolt queueing can reduce gaps, but never hide an assigned control spell inside the same key.
  • Report pet death when it removes Blood Pact or meaningful damage.

Failure to avoid: leaving the Imp on an immune target or in a hazard because pet follow was never bound.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Treating the short-cooldown priority as one unchanging cast sequence.
  • Attempting Conflagrate without Immolate or using the wrong glyph assumptions.
  • Spending Backdraft charges during forced movement or on pointless refreshes.
Raid mistakes
  • Using a full boss setup on a target that will die immediately.
  • Hiding Shadowfury inside a damage macro when it is an assigned stun.
  • Losing Imp damage and Blood Pact through uncontrolled pet positioning.
