HoT foundation

  • Tree of Life defines the healing form and reduces HoT mana costs.
  • Empowered Rejuvenation improves scaling for key periodic effects.
  • Swiftmend converts an active Rejuvenation or Regrowth into immediate triage.

Raid and tank tools

  • Wild Growth supplies efficient short-cooldown group healing.
  • Living Seed creates follow-up healing after critical direct heals.
  • Nature's Swiftness supports a planned instant Healing Touch emergency.

Self-contained mana

  • Omen of Clarity and Intensity provide value without assumed raid-only support.
  • Celestial Focus supplies 3% haste in the displayed 11/0/60 baseline.
  • If those Balance points move later, the haste model changes from roughly 735 to roughly 856 under the stated raid buffs.

Raid-frame information↑ Top

Show clearly

  • Health deficit and incoming heals.
  • Your Rejuvenation, Regrowth, and Lifebloom duration and stacks.
  • Curse and poison indicators, range, line of sight, and dead status.

Avoid noise

  • Do not hide dangerous debuffs under oversized HoT icons.
  • Separate your effects from other Druids where possible.
  • Test every click or mouseover binding outside the raid before relying on it.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Mouseover Rejuvenation

Heal a living friendly mouseover, then a friendly target, then self without changing targets.

#showtooltip Rejuvenation /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Rejuvenation

Mouseover Swiftmend

Use immediate triage on a living friendly mouseover or target.

#showtooltip Swiftmend /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Swiftmend

Mouseover Remove Curse

Cleanse a friendly mouseover without changing the healing target.

#showtooltip Remove Curse /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Remove Curse

Mouseover Rebirth

Combat-resurrect a dead friendly mouseover or target.

#showtooltip Rebirth /cast [@mouseover,help,dead][help,dead] Rebirth

Mouseover Innervate

Give mana to a living friendly mouseover, current friendly target, or self.

#showtooltip Innervate /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Innervate
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for incoming-damage and movement timing.
  • 3.3.5-compatible raid frames that display your HoT durations, Lifebloom stacks, debuffs, range, and incoming heals.
  • A mana and cooldown display visible beside the raid frames rather than at the edge of the screen.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Clearcasting, Wild Growth, Swiftmend, and Innervate.
  • Details/Recount to review deaths, target coverage, overhealing, dispels, mana use, and cooldown timing.
  • Clique or tested mouseover macros for direct bindings; never install a binding package without verifying original-client behavior.
