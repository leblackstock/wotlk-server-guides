Demo Lock Playing Guide

Run the DoT and filler priority, react to Molten Core and Decimation, and use the Felguard and Metamorphosis safely.

Combat enginePlaybooksCooldownsMistakesSources

Core decision engine↑ Top

Felguard

Keep the Felguard alive, attacking the correct target, and empowered without wasting the cooldown while it travels.

Procs

Switch fillers deliberately: Shadow Bolt by default, Incinerate for Molten Core, and Soul Fire for Decimation.

Metamorphosis

Choose a safe full window first; proximity damage is optional and never outranks mechanics.

Demonic Pact

Build reliable personal spell power and pet uptime before optimizing raid-wide proc timing.

Default priority: Fel Armor and Glyph of Life Tap buff → Metamorphosis in a useful planned window → assigned curse → Corruption → Immolate → Shadow Bolt filler → Incinerate during Molten Core → Soul Fire during Decimation.

Opener

  • Use a safe pre-pull Life Tap when Glyph of Life Tap is equipped; start with Fel Armor and the Felguard ready.
  • Precast Shadow Bolt when the tank and pull timer allow it, then send the Felguard.
  • Apply the assigned curse, Corruption, and Immolate.
  • Use Metamorphosis early only when the full window will connect; proximity abilities are a separate safety decision.

Track this

  • Corruption, Immolate, assigned curse, Molten Core, and Decimation.
  • Metamorphosis, Demonic Empowerment, Glyph of Life Tap, mana, health, and threat.
  • Felguard target, health, range, Cleave, and movement path.
  • Demonic Pact presence as a diagnostic; reliable pet uptime comes before proc micromanagement.

Ordinary target

Run the base priority

Default

Decision

Maintain the required effects and use Shadow Bolt while no stronger proc state is active.

Corruption›Immolate›Shadow Bolt
  • Corruption remains valuable because it can trigger Molten Core.
  • Use Curse of Doom only when it has time to complete; otherwise choose the assigned utility or shorter damage curse.

Failure to avoid: casting Incinerate without Molten Core or losing Corruption while waiting for a proc.

Molten Core active

Switch to Incinerate

Proc

Decision

Use the proc on Incinerate while maintaining higher-priority effects.

Molten Core›Incinerate›Immolate
  • Do not let the proc distract from an urgent target switch or expiring effect.
  • If Decimation is active, Soul Fire takes precedence.

Failure to avoid: continuing Shadow Bolt through the proc or consuming the phase on a dying target.

Target below 35%

Soul Fire execute

Execute

Decision

Use instant-access Decimation procs to make Soul Fire the filler while required effects stay active.

Decimation›Soul Fire›Corruption
  • A low-health add can activate Decimation for damage on the assigned target.
  • Keep Corruption and Immolate only when their remaining ticks justify the globals.

Failure to avoid: ignoring Decimation or spamming Soul Fire after the proc has fallen.

Planned cooldown

Use Metamorphosis safely

Cooldown

Decision

Choose a window with continuous target access; add proximity abilities only if the position is safe.

Metamorphosis›Immolation Aura›Shadow Cleave
  • Metamorphosis still grants ranged damage even when melee range is unsafe.
  • Demon Charge costs a global and is a movement tool, not a mandatory opener.

Failure to avoid: charging into a lethal mechanic to chase optional Immolation Aura ticks.

Pet movement

Protect the Felguard

Pet

Decision

Recall or redirect before the pet crosses hazards, attacks an immune target, or misses the next assigned enemy.

Demonic Empowerment›Cleave›Intercept
  • Use Demonic Empowerment when the pet can actually attack.
  • Turn off automatic Intercept when an unintended charge would kill or misposition the pet.

Failure to avoid: losing both Felguard damage and Demonic Pact because follow was never bound.

Stacked enemies

Choose controlled AoE

AoE

Decision

Use Seed on durable stacks and Metamorphosis proximity tools only where tank threat and mechanics permit.

Seed of Corruption›Metamorphosis›Immolation Aura
  • Rain of Fire is a simpler ranged choice when Seeds will not detonate reliably.
  • Keep the Felguard on the approved target instead of letting Cleave break control.

Failure to avoid: opening at close range before threat exists or breaking crowd control with pet Cleave.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Treating Shadow Bolt, Incinerate, and Soul Fire as interchangeable fillers.
  • Letting Corruption fall and then wondering why Molten Core never appears.
  • Using Metamorphosis during forced downtime or an immediate target immunity.
Raid mistakes
  • Charging into melee range when ranged Metamorphosis was the safe choice.
  • Allowing the Felguard to die, idle, or attack an immune target.
  • Chasing a larger Demonic Pact number before personal setup and pet uptime are reliable.
