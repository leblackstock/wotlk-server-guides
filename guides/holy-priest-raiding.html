Holy Priest Raid Healer Guide

Raid scope: these are Holy Priest responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of the Frost Wyrm for general throughput.
  • Firecracker Salmon or Fish Feast for spell power; use an Intellect or haste alternative when the set needs it.
  • Runic Mana Potion for progression sustain; Potion of Wild Magic only when mana is already secure.
  • Candles, personal food, repaired gear, and enough glyphs for planned swaps.

Confirm before pull

  • Raid-healing or tank-support assignment.
  • Which player receives Guardian Spirit and which mechanic triggers it.
  • Who dispels magic, who removes diseases, and which debuffs must wait for positioning.
  • Shadowfiend and Hymn of Hope timing.
  • Valithria portal team, if applicable.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / utility

Keep Prayer of Mending moving through predictable raid damage, use Abolish Disease on Paralytic Toxin targets during Anub'arak, and preserve mana for the long final phase. On heroic, avoid panic-overhealing Penetrating Cold targets before assignments are clear.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid healing

Preload Prayer of Mending for Bone Slice and Bone Storm, heal Bone Spike targets immediately, and keep moving during Storm with instant spells. Reserve Guardian Spirit for an assigned tank or trapped player rather than reacting after lethal damage.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / dispel

Stay spread, step out of Death and Decay, dispel removable magic effects on assignment, and help stabilize add tanks during waves. In modes with Dominate Mind, keep fast control and dispel communication separate from ordinary raid triage.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicMarks / tank

Support the active tank and assigned Mark of the Fallen Champion targets. Do not blanket the raid simply because marks exist; use efficient direct heals, Prayer of Mending, and a planned Guardian Spirit as mark count rises.

Festergut and Rotface

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / tank / disease

On Festergut, prepare group burst for Pungent Blight and tank damage at high Inhale stacks. On Rotface, remove Mutated Infection only at the raid leader's called drop location; an early disease removal creates the ooze in the wrong place.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / utility

Heal through ooze and gas target pressure while moving early for Malleable Goo and slime pools. Unbound Plague on heroic is a transfer mechanic, not a routine dispel; follow the raid's handoff route and use cooldowns on the actual damage peak.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / tank

Support the Keleseth tank during empowered windows, spread for Shock Vortex, and move promptly for Pact or Swarming Shadows. Fear Ward can cover a planned fear target for Blood-Queen; do not let the fear interrupt assigned healing.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicPortal healerNeeds Hellscream test

If assigned to portals, collect every safe Dream Cloud stack, return in range, and pour stacked throughput into Valithria. Use Guardian Spirit for its healing-received bonus when the server permits it on the encounter target; verify Hellscream's exact interaction before planning around it.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRaid / tank

Manage Unchained Magic and Instability deliberately—stop casting before the stack becomes lethal. In phase three, use Ice Tombs to reset Mystic Buffet while remaining in range of the active tank and marked targets.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAll healing

Prepare for Infest, move before Defile, and remove Necrotic Plague only on the raid's explicit call and route. Coordinate Guardian Spirit with Soul Reaper tank cooldowns, keep Val'kyr victims alive, and enter transitions with mana tools already planned.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRealm healing

Stay with the assigned realm, move early for combustion or consumption drops, and keep the tank covered through cutter movement. In the twilight realm, position so Twilight Cutters do not force a long out-of-range sweep.
