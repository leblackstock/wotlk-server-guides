◆ Talents

Recommended baseline: use a 51/20/0 Holy/Protection raid build that reaches Beacon of Light, Divine Sacrifice, Divine Guardian, Improved Righteous Fury, and Improved Devotion Aura.

Standard 51/20/0 reference

Use the full Imp Devotion Aura raid build in the linked talent guide. It documents the core points and the few legitimate flex choices without sending you to an unverified calculator string.

Core Holy talents

  • Divine Intellect and Holy Guidance make Intellect the engine of both mana and spell power.
  • Illumination returns part of the base mana cost when healing spells crit.
  • Light's Grace keeps Holy Light at its practical cast speed after the first cast.
  • Judgements of the Pure supplies 15% haste for one minute after Judging.
  • Infusion of Light turns Holy Shock crits into mobile or emergency follow-up casts.
  • Beacon of Light defines the two-target healing pattern.

Protection subspec

  • Improved Devotion Aura supplies armor plus a raid healing modifier when the equivalent effect is not already covered.
  • Divine Sacrifice and Divine Guardian create a major raid cooldown and extend Sacred Shield.
  • Improved Righteous Fury grants 6% damage reduction while Righteous Fury is active.
  • Turn Righteous Fury off when healer threat on newly spawned adds is dangerous.

Flexible points

  • Blessed Hands improves Hand of Sacrifice and Hand of Salvation.
  • Improved Lay on Hands adds a long-cooldown physical mitigation effect, but competes with stronger frequent utility.
  • Improved Concentration Aura is niche where silence or pushback control matters.
  • Improved Blessing of Wisdom is useful only when your raid needs you to supply it.
Hellscream check: confirm the Divine Sacrifice cancel behavior and Sacred Shield duration extension before relying on private-server timing. Keep the standard build, but test the script.

◆ Macros

Mouseover heal

Replace the spell name to create Holy Light, Flash of Light, Holy Shock, or Cleanse variants.

#showtooltip Holy Light /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Holy Light

Beacon assignment

Mouseover first, then focus, then current friendly target.

#showtooltip Beacon of Light /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][@focus,help,nodead][help,nodead] Beacon of Light

Sacred Shield assignment

Keeps the shield on the intended tank without retargeting the boss.

#showtooltip Sacred Shield /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][@focus,help,nodead][help,nodead] Sacred Shield

Hand of Sacrifice

Fast external on a mouseover or focus tank.

#showtooltip Hand of Sacrifice /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][@focus,help,nodead][help,nodead] Hand of Sacrifice

Divine Guardian cancel

First press activates Divine Sacrifice; second press removes the transfer aura while the raid reduction remains on standard 3.3.5 behavior.

#showtooltip Divine Sacrifice /cast Divine Sacrifice /cancelaura Divine Sacrifice

Emergency self protection

Use before or with Hand of Sacrifice when transferred damage would threaten you.

#showtooltip Divine Protection /cast Divine Protection

Aura Mastery reminder

Aura Mastery boosts the aura currently active. Put the correct aura on a nearby keybind.

#showtooltip Aura Mastery /cast Aura Mastery /raid Aura Mastery active

Cancel Divine Shield

Use cautiously when an immunity must end immediately.

/cancelaura Divine Shield
Macro limit: macros can choose a target and cast one spell. They cannot decide who is actually in danger. Keep the brain installed.

◆ Addons and interface

Essential

  • DBM: predict damage and movement instead of reacting after the health bar folds.
  • PallyPower: coordinate blessings cleanly.
  • Raid frames or HealBot: show health, range, Beacon, Sacred Shield, incoming heals, and dispellable debuffs.

Recommended

  • Details or Recount: review deaths, casts, overhealing, cooldown use, and dispels rather than racing raw HPS.
  • WeakAuras or TellMeWhen: track Beacon, Sacred Shield, Judgements of the Pure, Divine Plea, and cooldowns.
  • Threat display: useful when Righteous Fury is active or adds spawn near healers.
