14/0/57 standard Shadow raid build

The damage engine is tight: core Shadow talents, Improved Inner Fire, Meditation, and Inner Focus leave only narrow flex around pushback protection, range, mana, and Silence.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Core damage

  • Shadowform, Vampiric Touch, Pain and Suffering, and Dispersion are non-negotiable.
  • Shadow Weaving and Twisted Faith define the opener and stat interactions.
  • Improved Devouring Plague, Darkness, and Shadow Power are direct damage.

Discipline value

  • Improved Inner Fire adds spell power as long as charges remain.
  • Meditation plus Spirit Tap improves sustain.
  • Inner Focus is a small free-cast tool and a legitimate one-point flex.

Real flex points

  • Improved Shadowform protects channels from pushback and lets Fade break movement impairing effects.
  • Focused Mind and Veiled Shadows trade mana and Shadowfiend frequency.
  • Silence is useful, but reaching it costs several points.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Mind Flay without target loss

Stop the channel manually; avoid a castsequence that hides priority decisions.

#showtooltip Mind Flay /cast [harm,nodead] Mind Flay

Mouseover Dispel Magic

Dispel an ally without dropping the boss target.

#showtooltip Dispel Magic /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead] Dispel Magic

Focus Silence

Interrupt the focus when talented; otherwise cast on the current hostile target.

#showtooltip Silence /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Silence

Self-or-mouseover Power Word: Shield

Emergency movement protection without changing target.

#showtooltip Power Word: Shield /cast [mod:alt,@player][@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Power Word: Shield
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for movement, add, and soak timing.
  • A 3.3.5-compatible DoT timer that shows tenths near expiration.
  • A cast/channel bar with latency and visible Mind Flay ticks.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for five Shadow Weaving stacks, Shadowfiend, Dispersion, and trinket procs.
  • Details/Recount to review DoT uptime, missed spells, channel clipping, target damage, and utility.
  • Decursive for assigned friendly magic removal without turning the DPS UI into healer clutter.
