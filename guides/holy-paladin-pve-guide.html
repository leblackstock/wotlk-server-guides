Core assignment
Direct-heal one target, Beacon the other
In two-tank damage, Beacon one tank and cast directly on the other. Do not habitually cast into your own Beacon unless that target alone is in danger.
Default healing spell
Holy Light
Holy Light is the normal raid workhorse. Flash of Light is the efficient low-damage tool, not a replacement for required burst healing.
Default major glyphs
Holy Light + Seal of Wisdom
Use Glyph of Holy Light and Glyph of Seal of Wisdom. The third slot is usually Beacon of Light or Divinity.
Healing engine
Maintain → cast → transfer → recover
Maintain

Beacon of Light, Sacred Shield, and Judgements of the Pure.

Direct heal

Cast Holy Light, Flash of Light, or Holy Shock on the target who needs the spell.

Transfer

Your eligible direct heal is copied to the Beacon target.

Recover

Plan Divine Plea, Seal of Wisdom contact, potions, and quiet windows.

Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard patch 3.3.5 behavior is the baseline. Beacon transfer, Sacred Shield, proc trinkets, encounter immunities, and scripted raid abilities may differ. Hellscream ICC also does not use the zone-wide ICC buff, so healing and mana checks are less forgiving than guides that assume it.

Before the pull

  • Beacon of Light is on the assigned target.
  • Sacred Shield is on the assigned tank.
  • Correct aura, blessing, and Seal of Wisdom are active.
  • Your Judgement target is reachable so Judgements of the Pure can be maintained.
  • Tank frames, Beacon timer, Sacred Shield timer, and debuffs are visible.
  • Divine Plea, Aura Mastery, Divine Guardian, Hands, Lay on Hands, and a mana potion are bound.

First gearing rule

  • Build a large mana pool. Intellect improves maximum mana and several percentage-based returns.
  • Cast fast enough. Haste is the strongest practical throughput stat once the mana base is stable.
  • Prefer reliable sustain. MP5 is steady; crit is useful but weaker regeneration than older guides imply.
  • Do not worship plate. Wear the best uncontested healing item your raid rules allow.
