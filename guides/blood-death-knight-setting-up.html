Blood DK Setup Guide

Talents: exact 43/23/5↑ Top

Respec baseline: 43 Blood / 23 Frost / 5 Unholy. This is the complete raid build, not a partial framework with flex points left over.
Fully filled baseline

43/23/5 LK25H personal-survival build

Before you respec, confirm that the 25-player roster supplies the 20% melee-haste buff. With that coverage secured, this tree takes the available single-target avoidance and Blood’s low-health safety net.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Blood: survival core

  • 5/5 Blade Barrier — 5% all-damage reduction while both native Blood runes are cooling down.
  • 3/3 Improved Rune Tap — doubles Rune Tap to 20% maximum health and reduces its cooldown to 30 sec.
  • 3/3 Spell Deflection — parry-chance proc that reduces direct spell damage by 45%.
  • 3/3 Veteran of the Third War — Strength, Stamina, and expertise.
  • 2/2 Improved Death Strike, Vampiric Blood, and 3/3 Will of the Necropolis.

Frost: mitigation and control

  • 3/3 Improved Icy Touch — 20% melee attack-speed reduction.
  • 5/5 Toughness — 15% more armor from items and shorter movement slows.
  • 5/5 Black Ice and 5/5 Icy Talons — threat/personal haste on the path.
  • 2/2 Endless Winter — Strength and a no-RP Mind Freeze.
  • 3/3 Frigid Dreadplate — 3% melee miss, not subject to dodge diminishing returns.

Unholy: raw avoidance

  • 5/5 Anticipation adds 5% dodge.
  • ICC’s Chill of the Throne subtracts 20 percentage points of dodge; it does not invalidate Anticipation.
  • The tree does not take Virulence. Compensate taunt reliability with hit and Glyph of Dark Command.

Selected threat points

5/5 Bladed Armor, 1/3 Scent of Blood, 5/5 Dark Conviction, 2/2 Two-Handed Weapon Specialization, 3/3 Bloody Vengeance, 2/2 Abomination's Might, and Hysteria provide pathing, Runic Power, personal threat, and raid damage. They are not guaranteed mitigation. Death Rune Mastery converts the Frost/Unholy runes spent by a successful Death Strike into Death runes when they reactivate; after a converted rune is spent, it returns to its original type unless converted again.

Deliberately skipped

No Heart Strike, Improved Blood Presence, Mark of Blood, Bloodworms, Might of Mograine, Blood Gorged, Dancing Rune Weapon, Lichborne, Improved Icy Talons, Morbidity, Bone Shield, or Anti-Magic Zone. Those points buy passive healing, damage, AoE convenience, raid buff coverage, or different toolkits—not more reliable single-target survival than this locked build. When you and the raid leader write the cooldown sheet, exclude Mark of Blood, Lichborne healing, Bone Shield, and AMZ; this character cannot supply them.

Flex and alternatives: the exact tree has no unspent points. Its 1/3 Scent of Blood is the lowest-impact selected point, but moving it produces a different locked tree. Use 43/26/2 only when the 25-player roster lacks both an Enhancement Shaman and a Frost DK providing the 20% melee-haste buff; it gives up avoidance to cover the raid buff. A common 53/8/10-style threat build spends deeper in Blood for Heart Strike/Might of Mograine/Blood Gorged and does not reach Frigid Dreadplate or free Mind Freeze. Neither is the best personal-survival baseline for this job.

Minor glyphs

Glyph of Blood Tap removes Blood Tap’s self-damage; Glyph of Pestilence increases its radius; Glyph of Raise Dead removes the reagent requirement. These three are the clean baseline. Glyph of Horn of Winter only extends duration; it does not improve the buff and is not preferred over these three.

Macros↑ Top

Audit before replacing. Keep an existing raid macro when it preserves target control, exposes failures, and leaves every defensive on its own decision. Use the examples below to repair gaps, not to erase a setup you already execute reliably.

Primary taunt: mouseover → focus → target

Taunt the intended unit without changing the boss target.

#showtooltip Dark Command /cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][@focus,harm,nodead][] Dark Command

Backup taunt / grip

Use as the second taunt when Dark Command resists; movement only occurs on grippable targets.

#showtooltip Death Grip /cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][@focus,harm,nodead][] Death Grip

Focus interrupt

Interrupt an assigned add without dropping the boss target.

#showtooltip Mind Freeze /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Mind Freeze

Emergency ghoul heal

First press summons if needed; press again after the global cooldown to sacrifice the ghoul.

#showtooltip Death Pact /cast [nopet] Raise Dead /cast Death Pact

Hysteria on named DPS

Replace NAME once; never hide this raid cooldown inside a threat button.

#showtooltip Hysteria /cast [@NAME,help,nodead] Hysteria

Emergency announce

Ask for the named external while using the personal cooldown.

#showtooltip Icebound Fortitude /cast Icebound Fortitude /say IBF ACTIVE — NEXT EXTERNAL ON ME

Manual Rune Strike queue

Queue or cancel Rune Strike from its own bind; do not hide it inside every strike.

#showtooltip Rune Strike /cast !Rune Strike

Rune Tap

Keep the off-global heal independent so it can be timed after actual damage.

#showtooltip Rune Tap /cast Rune Tap

Vampiric Blood

Keep the maximum-health/healing cooldown separate from Rune Tap.

#showtooltip Vampiric Blood /cast Vampiric Blood

Anti-Magic Shell

Spend 20 RP on the exact magic window without coupling it to IBF.

#showtooltip Anti-Magic Shell /cast Anti-Magic Shell

Blood Tap / T10 layer

Activate the rune and, with four tank-tier pieces, the tracked 12% reduction.

#showtooltip Blood Tap /cast Blood Tap

Empower Rune Weapon

Make the five-minute rune/RP reset a deliberate standalone press.

#showtooltip Empower Rune Weapon /cast Empower Rune Weapon

Pet safety

Pull the ghoul out of an unsafe target or frontal before an encounter handoff.

#showtooltip Raise Dead /petpassive /petfollow
Player macro limits: keep Rune Strike out of every-strike macros because an automatic queue can leave IBF or AMS unusable. Do not retain a Mark of Blood macro from another build; the talent is not selected. Keep Army of the Dead off generic combat binds and pair its dedicated key with the raid leader’s approved warning, because its channel costs one rune of each type and its ghouls can taunt.

Audit your raid interface↑ Top

You do not need a new UI because you changed specs. Your existing UI passes this audit when it exposes the information below early enough for you to act; the named addons are examples, not requirements.

Boss and threat

  • DBM or the raid’s approved boss mod: Soul Reaper, Defile, transition, Harvest Souls, and Vile Spirits timers.
  • Omen: threat lead. TauntMaster or an equivalent 3.3.5 taunt display: co-tank target, taunt cooldown, and resist/failure visibility.
  • Details/Recount: post-pull death log, cooldown usage, and heal timing—not mid-pull meter watching.

DK resources

  • Magic Runes, Engraved, or an equivalent 3.3.5 rune display: show type, recharge, and Death-rune state.
  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen/NeedToKnow in a verified 3.3.5-compatible build: diseases, Blade Barrier, Vampiric Blood, IBF, AMS, Blood Tap/T10, Rune Tap, and external cooldowns.
  • A large 20-RP warning when IBF is ready but not castable.
  • A boss swing bar or combat-timer display beside Soul Reaper so the hasted-melee window is visible.

Raid frames

  • Show healer deaths, external cooldowns on you, Necrotic Plague, Harvest Souls, and target-of-target.
  • Keep the co-tank frame next to the boss frame with range, debuffs, and incoming resurrection visible.
  • Use clear audio for taunt resist, Soul Reaper application, and your named external.

Final bind and tracking audit↑ Top

One movement-hand reach

Dark Command, Death Grip, Icy Touch, Death Strike, Rune Strike, Rune Tap, Icebound Fortitude, Anti-Magic Shell, Vampiric Blood, Healthstone, Indestructible Potion, Mind Freeze, and the external-call macro.

Always visible

Current/maximum health, incoming heals, six runes by original type, Runic Power, boss swing, diseases, Blade Barrier, all personal cooldowns, trinkets, potion/Healthstone, co-tank taunt status, and the next three encounter timers.
