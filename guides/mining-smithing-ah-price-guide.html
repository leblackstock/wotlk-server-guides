    Tradeable Mining smelts and conversions↑ Top

    This catalog covers 24 unique tradeable outputs from the standard WotLK 3.3.5 Mining spell list: 22 Mining-owned bars and alloys with completed Phase 1A Evidence Pricing decisions plus two exact reversible 10:1 elemental breakdowns. The Phase 2 closeout revalidated every saved band, recipe, duplicate, note, and conversion without fetching a redundant second market snapshot or changing a price. Titanium Bar and Enchanted Thorium Bar remain related references because their identical items are owned by the canonical Alchemy and Enchanting catalogs; the other 16 Mining ranks, tracking, smelting headers, and Toughness passives create no tradeable output. Exact 3.3.5 recipe inputs, ratios, guaranteed output, and saved non-circular values remain separate craftability diagnostics. No active Hellscream listing value sets either layer.

    * Evidence Pricing and smelting cost: Twenty-two bars and alloys retain their completed Phase 1A Evidence Pricing decisions; all had three-realm relative-rank coverage, external gold was not copied, and active Hellscream listings never set value. Mote of Fire and Mote of Earth retain exact reversible 10:1 conversion pricing without a convenience markup. Exact 3.3.5 input ratios and guaranteed output remain separate craftability diagnostics: five market estimates fall below at least one matching floor, so do not buy those inputs to smelt at the saved sale values. Standard 3.3.5 data shows no Titansteel cooldown; verify Hellscream before charging for server-specific access.
