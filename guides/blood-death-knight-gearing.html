Level-80 gearing path

1. Become legal

540 Defense skill → tank stance → Runeforging.

Use crafted, reputation, dungeon, and emblem pieces without caring about a fashionable set. Re-check the floor after every weapon, trinket, or tier change.

2. Build a raid base

Health + armor → reliable taunt → stable threat.

Farm stamina trinkets and bonus-armor pieces. Reach enough hit that Dark Command and Icy Touch are dependable with the actual raid debuffs.

3. Build T10 control

Work toward four tank-tier pieces.

T10 4-piece turns Blood Tap into a 12%-reduction, 10-second cooldown layer. Keep high-armor offsets for the fifth slot and alternate sets.

4. No-buff LK set

Maximize worst-case effective health.

Use stamina/armor trinkets, a high-stamina slow 2H, and enough hit for the planned taunt model. Avoid gear that relies on stacks staying up.

5. Add-tank set

Physical smoothing + control.

Favor armor and predictable on-use mitigation for Shambling Horror Enrage/Frenzy. Keep hit for pickups and do not assume a proc will be active.

6. Magic/soak set

Health + magic cooldowns.

Favor stamina and Sindragosa’s resistance on-use for Shadow/Frost-heavy jobs; do not overvalue armor for pure Vile Spirit damage.

Keep three complete swap sets

Boss / Soul Reaper

  • Maximum credible health.
  • High armor without exceeding peak cap.
  • Taunt reliability with the actual raid debuff.
  • Predictable on-use trinket plan.

Shambling / physical

  • Bonus armor and physical trinkets.
  • Pickup hit/expertise where inexpensive.
  • Stun/control coordination.
  • Never depend on an Unidentifiable Organ stack during avoidance streaks.

Magic / Vile Spirits

  • Stamina and resistance on-use.
  • AMS timer and absorb cap visible.
  • Armor is secondary against pure Shadow burst.
  • Keep 540 Defense if melee contact is possible.

Physical EHP

  • Bonus armor plus Stamina.
  • Check all armor procs against 49,905.
  • Use for LK melee or Horrors, not pure Shadow jobs.

Threat / pickup

  • Slow high-DPS two-hander.
  • Hit/expertise only where inexpensive.
  • Preserve 540 Defense and the assigned survival floor.

Taunt reliability

  • Glyph of Dark Command.
  • 237 spell-hit rating alone or 158 with a confirmed 3% debuff.
  • Re-check every swap; never assume debuff uptime.
