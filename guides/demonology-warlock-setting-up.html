0/56/15 Fresh-80 self-contained Demonology baseline

Use the complete Felguard and Metamorphosis baseline. The character works without an assumed external caster debuff or raid support package.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Pet foundation

  • Summon Felguard supplies the specialization's primary pet.
  • Demonic Knowledge turns inherited pet stats into personal spell power.
  • Demonic Empowerment is a frequent off-global pet cooldown.

Proc engine

  • Molten Core turns Corruption ticks into stronger, faster fire fillers.
  • Decimation makes Soul Fire the execute filler below 35%.
  • Demonic Pact converts reliable Felguard criticals into raid support.

Real flex points

  • Pet healing, pushback, and threat options can move for a known environment.
  • Early hit talents are an explicit alternate build and change the gear requirement; they are not silently assumed here.
  • Any swap must preserve the complete Felguard, Demonic Pact, and Metamorphosis engine.

Felguard control↑ Top

Damage controls

  • Bind attack, follow, passive, Cleave, and Intercept.
  • Use Demonic Empowerment when the Felguard has target uptime.
  • Pet criticals maintain Demonic Pact; an idle pet provides neither damage nor support.

Survival controls

  • Recall before transitions, void zones, and unsafe pathing.
  • Disable automatic Intercept when a charge would cross mechanics.
  • Resummon only when the current assignment can spare the cast and shard.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Empowered Shadow Bolt

Use the ordinary filler, command the Felguard, and activate Demonic Empowerment when available.

#showtooltip Shadow Bolt /petattack [harm,nodead] /cast Demonic Empowerment /cast Shadow Bolt

Controlled Soul Fire

Use the execute filler without changing the Felguard's selected target automatically.

#showtooltip Soul Fire /cast Soul Fire

Stop and follow

Recall the Felguard immediately from a hazard or immune target.

/petpassive /petfollow

Demonic Circle return

Exit a planned proximity window or mechanic through the pre-placed circle.

#showtooltip Demonic Circle: Teleport /cast Demonic Circle: Teleport
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for Metamorphosis access, movement, and execute timing.
  • A pet frame with clear health, target, range, and action feedback.
  • A 3.3.5-compatible proc display for Molten Core and Decimation.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Molten Core, Decimation, Metamorphosis, and Demonic Empowerment.
  • Details/Recount to review player and Felguard damage, pet target uptime, misses, and execute usage.
  • A threat display for planned Soulshatter before Metamorphosis or execute windows.
