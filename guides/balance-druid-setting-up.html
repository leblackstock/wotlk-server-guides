55/0/16 Fresh-80 self-contained Eclipse baseline

Use the fully allocated dungeon-and-solo baseline for complete Eclipse, Starfall, hit, and mana support. Treat raid-only damage swaps as later progression.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Eclipse engine

  • Eclipse creates the alternating Lunar and Solar damage windows.
  • Nature's Grace rewards critical casts with haste.
  • Starfall and Force of Nature provide planned cooldown damage.

Self-contained hit

  • Balance of Power supplies 4% spell hit and Improved Faerie Fire supplies 3% when its debuff is active.
  • Against a level-83 raid target, the baseline therefore seeks 10%—about 263 rating—from gear before counting another player’s effects.
  • That raid target is progression, not a normal-dungeon entry gate.

Dungeon support

  • Intensity and Omen of Clarity make the baseline functional without assumed raid-only mana support.
  • Later raid swaps must state which self-contained tool is removed and why.
  • Preserve 71 allocated points whenever a flex choice is tested.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Mouseover Innervate

Give mana to a living friendly mouseover, otherwise use the current friendly target or self.

#showtooltip Innervate /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead][@player] Innervate

Focus Entangling Roots

Root a hostile focus without losing the damage target; fall back to the current target.

#showtooltip Entangling Roots /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Entangling Roots

Mouseover Rebirth

Combat-resurrect a dead friendly mouseover without changing the current target.

#showtooltip Rebirth /cast [@mouseover,help,dead][help,dead] Rebirth

Form-safe Barkskin

Use personal mitigation without leaving Moonkin Form.

#showtooltip Barkskin /cast Barkskin
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for movement and cooldown timing.
  • A 3.3.5-compatible Eclipse display that shows the active buff, the available trigger direction, and remaining duration.
  • A DoT timer that keeps Moonfire and Insect Swarm distinct on the selected target.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Eclipse, Nature's Grace, Starfall, and Innervate.
  • Details/Recount to review DoT uptime, Eclipse casts, misses, target damage, and avoidable deaths.
  • A threat display so cooldown windows do not surprise the tank.
