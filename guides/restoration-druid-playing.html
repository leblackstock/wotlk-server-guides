Resto Druid Playing Guide

Choose between HoTs, Lifebloom, Wild Growth, Swiftmend, Nourish, movement, mana, and recovery tools by damage pattern.

Core decision engine↑ Top

HoTs

Pre-HoT predictable damage with Rejuvenation and Regrowth while avoiding wasteful blanket healing in small groups.

Lifebloom

Choose deliberately between rolling a stack, letting it bloom, and using Clearcasting for efficient healing and mana return.

Raid healing

Use Wild Growth for real clustered damage and Swiftmend for urgent recovery while preserving assignment coverage.

Mana and procs

Track mana, Clearcasting, Innervate, Revitalize, and expensive direct heals so recovery tools remain available.

Default priority: Pre-place Rejuvenation and assigned Regrowth before known damage → use Wild Growth on injured groups → Swiftmend a HoT target needing immediate help → maintain assigned Lifebloom stacks deliberately → use Nourish or Regrowth for direct triage → use Nature's Swiftness and Healing Touch for a planned emergency.

Opener

  • Pre-HoT the tank with Rejuvenation and Regrowth only close enough to the pull that useful duration remains.
  • Use Lifebloom according to the tank assignment; refresh the stack intentionally or allow a planned bloom for mana return and healing.
  • Use Wild Growth after real group damage or just ahead of a known clustered hit.
  • Preserve Swiftmend and the Nature's Swiftness emergency for actual triage rather than spending every tool at the pull.

Track this

  • Rejuvenation, Regrowth, and Lifebloom by target and remaining duration.
  • Wild Growth, Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Tranquility, and Barkskin.
  • Mana, Innervate, Clearcasting, potion use, and the expected remaining encounter time.
  • Incoming-damage timers, range, movement, curses, poisons, and the assigned Rebirth target.

Steady tank damage

Build a sustainable tank layer

Tank

Decision

Maintain the assigned HoTs and use direct healing only when incoming damage exceeds their coverage.

Rejuvenation›Regrowth›Lifebloom›Nourish
  • Refresh Lifebloom intentionally; a bloom and mana return can be correct when planned.
  • Nourish gains value from existing HoTs but should still answer real damage.

Failure to avoid: rolling an expensive stack without regard for mana, target swaps, or the next damage event.

Sudden injury

Land the next safe heal

Triage

Decision

Use Swiftmend on an eligible HoT target, then choose Nourish, Regrowth, or the emergency based on time to death.

Swiftmend›Nourish›Nature's Swiftness›Healing Touch
  • The fastest available button is not automatically the smallest sufficient answer.
  • Do not consume Nature's Swiftness for damage ordinary triage can cover.

Failure to avoid: continuing blanket HoTs while one assigned target will die before the next tick.

Group damage

Heal the cluster that is hurt

Raid

Decision

Place Wild Growth on a target whose nearby group will receive useful jumps, then Rejuvenate players with continuing danger.

Wild Growth›Rejuvenation›Swiftmend
  • Target position matters; do not assume the selected unit represents the injured cluster.
  • Blanket only the players and duration supported by the actual damage pattern.

Failure to avoid: casting Wild Growth on an isolated player or covering safe groups while the damaged group falls.

Forced movement

Move while preserving triage

Mobile

Decision

Use instant heals, Swiftmend, utility, or Barkskin while moving and choose the destination before leaving range.

Rejuvenation›Swiftmend›Barkskin
  • Move early so the mechanic does not force a panic route.
  • Do not refresh every HoT early merely because an instant global is available.

Failure to avoid: ending movement out of range, out of line of sight, or without an emergency global.

Mana pressure

Recover before the dry phase

Mana

Decision

Reduce low-value coverage, use Clearcasting intelligently, and activate Innervate early enough for full value.

Clearcasting›Innervate›Lifebloom
  • A free cast should answer a useful healing need, not manufacture overheal.
  • Match Lifebloom maintenance to the assignment instead of rolling every available tank.

Failure to avoid: maintaining maximum coverage until mana reaches zero and recovery comes too late.

Death or collapse

Recover the raid deliberately

Recovery

Decision

Stabilize living players, resurrect the agreed target in a safe location, and use Tranquility only where its channel can complete usefully.

Rebirth›Tranquility›Barkskin
  • Confirm the target and healing follow-up before Rebirth.
  • Use Barkskin before a vulnerable Tranquility channel when incoming damage is expected.

Failure to avoid: starting a long recovery cast while the remaining tank or healer is about to die.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Blanketing safe targets until mana becomes the emergency.
  • Refreshing Lifebloom automatically instead of deciding whether the stack should roll or bloom.
  • Holding Swiftmend and Nature's Swiftness until after the assigned target dies.
Raid mistakes
  • Casting Wild Growth on a unit positioned away from the injured cluster.
  • Moving late and ending outside healing range or line of sight.
  • Starting Rebirth or Tranquility before stabilizing the players who must survive the cast.
