Emblems and tier↑ Top

Triumph

  • Use Talisman of Resurgence if trinkets are poor.
  • Build T9 Shadow tier while repairing weak armor.
  • Recalculate hit after every purchase.

Frost

  • Work toward T10 Shadow bonuses.
  • Buy strong offset pieces when they solve a weak slot without breaking set bonuses.
  • Do not bank emblems while wearing severe gaps.

Tier logic

  • Evaluate two-piece and four-piece bonuses as part of the whole set.
  • Keep hit alternatives in the bank.
  • A lower-item-level tier piece can beat a higher isolated offset when the bonus is strong.
