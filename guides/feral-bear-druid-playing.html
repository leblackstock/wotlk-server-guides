Feral Bear Playing Guide

Manage threat, Rage, Maul queues, Lacerate, packs, taunts, cooldowns, movement, and utility as one tanking system.

Core decision engine↑ Top

Forms

Enter Dire Bear Form before contact and change form only when the tank assignment and incoming damage permit it.

Resources

Generate Rage safely and queue Maul only when the next required Mangle, Lacerate, Swipe, or control global remains funded.

Bleeds

Maintain Lacerate on durable targets and use Mangle to support threat and bleed damage without losing pack control.

Survival

Plan mitigation, health buffers, recovery, taunts, stuns, and emergency pack control before the damage spike arrives.

Default priority: Dire Bear Form and group buffs → position enemies safely → Faerie Fire (Feral) at range → Mangle (Bear) → maintain Lacerate and required Demoralizing Roar → Swipe (Bear) for packs or filler → queue Maul only when Rage remains for the next required action.

Opener

  • Pull with Faerie Fire (Feral) or use Feral Charge - Bear only when the landing position is safe.
  • Face enemies away from the group, establish Mangle (Bear), and use Swipe (Bear) while marking the priority target.
  • Build Lacerate on a durable target and apply Demoralizing Roar when the physical-damage reduction is assigned.
  • Queue Maul only after reserving Rage for taunt recovery, interrupts, debuffs, and the next pack action.

Track this

  • Threat on every active enemy, the current kill target, Growl, and Challenging Roar.
  • Rage, Mangle (Bear), Lacerate, Demoralizing Roar, and Faerie Fire (Feral).
  • Savage Defense, health, healer mana, Barkskin, Survival Instincts, and Frenzied Regeneration.
  • Boss swing or burst timers, tank-swap debuffs, frontal direction, and safe charge paths.

Single durable target

Build reliable threat

Default

Decision

Maintain the required debuffs and Lacerate while keeping enough Rage for reactions.

Faerie Fire (Feral)›Mangle (Bear)›Lacerate›Maul
  • Mangle has priority over filler when available.
  • Queue Maul only when it cannot starve the next required action.

Failure to avoid: spending every Rage point on Maul and missing a taunt, debuff, or pickup.

Enemy pack

Position before accelerating

Adds

Decision

Gather enemies into a stable frontal pack, spread threat, and keep the marked target obvious.

Faerie Fire (Feral)›Swipe (Bear)›Challenging Roar
  • Use line of sight to bring casters to the pack.
  • Challenging Roar creates a recovery window; it does not establish durable threat by itself.

Failure to avoid: charging past the group, exposing frontals, or assuming one Swipe owns every enemy.

Low Rage

Protect required actions

Resource

Decision

Stop queueing Maul, use Enrage only when the armor penalty is safe, and rebuild through incoming or dealt damage.

Enrage›Mangle (Bear)›Swipe (Bear)
  • Never treat Enrage as free Rage during a dangerous physical burst.
  • Prioritize control and debuffs over filler damage.

Failure to avoid: using Enrage into lethal melee damage or continuing to drain Rage with Maul.

Loose enemy

Recover deliberately

Pickup

Decision

Taunt the correct target, force immediate follow-up threat, and restore safe positioning.

Growl›Feral Charge - Bear›Mangle (Bear)
  • Taunt copies threat temporarily; follow it with a real threat action.
  • Charge only when the path and new facing are safe.

Failure to avoid: taunting without follow-up or turning the pack's frontal attacks through the group.

Physical burst

Layer survival early

Survival

Decision

Use the smallest sufficient cooldown before the burst and communicate what remains available.

Barkskin›Survival Instincts›Frenzied Regeneration
  • Survival Instincts is temporary health and needs a plan for expiration.
  • Frenzied Regeneration converts Rage, so account for the threat and control cost.

Failure to avoid: pressing every cooldown after health is already unrecoverable.

Magic or special damage

Use the right defense

Special

Decision

Favor health, timing, movement, resistance, or external help because armor does not solve most spell bursts.

Survival Instincts›Barkskin›Feral Charge - Bear
  • Identify the damage school before choosing gear or cooldowns.
  • Call for an external cooldown before the hit, not afterward.

Failure to avoid: equating high armor with complete protection from magical mechanics.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Looking only at the current target's threat while another enemy attacks a healer.
  • Queueing Maul until no Rage remains for control or recovery.
  • Charging into a position that turns frontal attacks toward the group.
Raid mistakes
  • Treating Challenging Roar as permanent threat rather than a short recovery window.
  • Using Enrage during dangerous physical damage without accounting for its armor penalty.
  • Waiting for critical health before communicating or activating survival cooldowns.
