Blood DK Heroic LK25 Tank Playbook

Use separate player and raid-leader calls for Hellscream with the Icecrown zone buff disabled. The raid leader names the exact tank job; the player equips and executes the matching set, resources, and cooldown sheet.

Soul Reaper: split the two threats↑ Top

Standard heroic record: the application deals 50% weapon damage; after 5 sec the carrier takes 70,000 Shadow damage and the Lich King gains 100% attack speed for 5 sec. The boss remains tauntable.

Player: carrier

  1. Call “Reaper on me.”
  2. Use the assigned personal before the delayed tick.
  3. Anti-Magic Shell just before the Shadow tick when assigned.
  4. Do not taunt back during the boss haste window.
  5. Death Strike/Rune Tap after damage if still tanking another target.

Player: swap tank

  1. Be in range with a clean path.
  2. Taunt after Reaper is applied, before the 5-sec detonation.
  3. Follow with Icy Touch; call a resist immediately.
  4. Use mitigation for the hasted melee window.
  5. Return the boss only on the agreed call.

Raid leader and healers

  1. Assign one external to a named tank/window.
  2. Do not stack every external on the carrier while the new tank eats hasted swings.
  3. Healers pre-target both tanks.
  4. Keep Defile movement from breaking taunt range.
  5. Record every failure by carrier vs swap tank.

Pull and Phase 1: 100% → 70%
All modesLK tank
Position for raid space, not personal comfort. Face LK away, keep the add pack separate, and leave a predictable lane toward the transition edge. Maintain Frost Fever and move the boss only on the raid’s trap/space call.
25 heroicShadow Trap
Move the boss away from every activated Shadow Trap. Heroic traps are summoned about every 15 sec, arm after roughly 5 sec, deal Shadow damage, and knock nearby players off the platform. Never drag the boss backward through melee; strafe on the established route.
25 heroicPlague Siphon
Escalate cooldowns with Plague Siphon. Every Necrotic Plague jump gives LK +2% physical damage. Save major coverage for the high-stack end of P1; a static “use at 30% health” rule is too late.
25 heroicShambling Horror
Collect three-Drudge waves and each minute Horror without turning the frontal. Horrors hit hard, cast a 150%-swing Shockwave in a 20-yard frontal about every 20 sec, Enrage for triple damage, and Frenzy below 20%. Call every Enrage for Tranquilizing Shot/Anesthetic Poison II and every low-health stun chain.
Plague anchorAdd tank
Make the closest valid jump target an add, never a player. The plagued player enters the assigned 10-yard transfer point before dispel. Do not chase the player with a Horror frontal. Keep enough Drudge Ghouls alive to feed the plague.
InfestVerify on Hellscream
Heroic Infest starts around 17.7–19.8k Shadow and ramps until health exceeds 90%. The player should report whether AMS only reduces the hit or also changes debuff application; the raid leader should keep AMS on its assigned tank burst until that behavior is confirmed.
First intermission: 70%
MovementBoth tanks
Move outside the 45-yard Remorseless Winter radius immediately. Heroic Winter deals roughly 15k Frost per second, while Pain and Suffering pressures stacked players. Finish the add/plague cleanup only from the safe edge; AMS is a bridge, not permission to stay inside.
25 heroicRaging Spirit
Pick up each Spirit instantly and face its 15-yard Soul Shriek cone away. Three spawn in the first intermission. Heroic Soul Shriek deals about 42.4–47.6k Shadow and silences for 5 sec; keep healers and the other tank outside the face.
Ice SphereQuake
Never let a Spirit pickup cross an Ice Sphere path. Its Ice Pulse marks the danger and Ice Burst knocks nearby players from the platform. At the end of the minute, Quake gives about 5 sec to return to the main platform. Move the Spirit with you without rotating Soul Shriek through the raid.
Phase 2: 70% → 40%
Val’kyrDefile
Hold LK near center for the triple-Val’kyr stack, then drag him with the raid’s cleave path. As Defile approaches, preserve a clean escape lane and never rotate the boss through the stack. Defile is roughly every 35 sec; Val’kyr about every 45 sec.
Soul ReaperBoth tanks
Execute the written carrier/swap split every ~30 sec. Carrier owns the 70k Shadow tick; swap tank owns the following five seconds of 100% boss attack speed. Call taunt success, not just the button press.
Carry-over SpiritTransition tank
Cleave a surviving Raging Spirit beside LK only with safe facing. The Spirit’s front remains more important than the cleave angle. Do not place it where a Defile move crosses its cone.
Timer driftVerify on Hellscream
The raid leader should record actual Soul Reaper, Defile, and Val’kyr timestamps for at least three representative pulls. Server scripting and push timing can change overlaps; write the cooldown sheet from observed Hellscream events.
Second intermission: 40%
25 heroicFour Spirits
Plan ownership before four Raging Spirits exist. Alternate pickups or stack them only with explicit major coverage. Keep every Soul Shriek facing aligned away from the raid and the other tank.
Resource resetP3 prep
Enter P3 with the Reaper rotation executable. Build RP above 20, refresh Horn, ensure diseases can be applied, and do not spend ERW merely to finish a Spirit unless assigned.
Phase 3: 40% → 10%
Soul ReaperDefile
Continue Reaper swaps while protecting Defile space. P3 removes Infest but keeps Reaper (~30 sec) and Defile (~35 sec). A correct taunt that drags LK across Defile is still a wipe; pre-position both tanks.
Vile SpiritsSoaker
Soak only the assigned wave/count. Ten spirits spawn; each heroic Spirit Burst is roughly 36k Shadow in 5 yards. Use Anti-Magic Shell/Sindragosa’s Flawless Fang first for magic, Icebound Fortitude or an external for overlap, and keep explosions away from the raid. Multiple simultaneous bursts can exceed any DK cooldown.
Harvest SoulsWhole raid
Survive the six-second raid channel, then execute the 40-second Frostmourne room. Standard heroic Harvest Souls deals 4k Shadow per second before transport. Inside, move as one group, kill Wicked Spirits, and sidestep the bombs that fall where they die.
ReturnTank priority
Leave Frostmourne with a tank assignment, not a discussion. The return group is stacked and Defile can follow quickly: spread on control, taunt the intended target, turn it away, and call any late death. One late interior death can give LK a +500% damage stack for 15 sec.
Return timingVerify on Hellscream
On each return, the player should call the target and threat state while the raid leader records the Defile timer and Vile Spirit activation. Use your own core’s observed exit order, not another group’s assumptions.
10% scripted finish
All rolesVictory event
Fury of Frostmourne is supposed to kill the raid. At 10%, it deals 1,000,000 Shadow damage and begins the scripted finish. Do not interpret the death as a defensive failure, release, or burn irreplaceable consumables trying to live through it.

Player reports and raid-leader validation↑ Top

Player: report from early pulls

  • Confirm whether the ICC zone buff is absent from both you and your pet.
  • Call the observed Soul Reaper application, delayed hit, haste, and successful taunt.
  • Report what AMS changes on Soul Reaper and Infest.
  • Report Dark Command and Death Grip results in every phase and on room return.
  • Call observed Shadow Trap arm timing, targeting, radius, and persistence.
  • Report every ghoul or Army taunt against LK, Horrors, Raging Spirits, or Vile Spirits.

Raid leader: log and lock

  • Record Nitro Boost failure modes inside ICC before assigning them to a critical move.
  • Lock the pre-pot/in-combat potion rule and verify armor-cap peaks.
  • Set the Vile Spirit hitbox, activation delay, burst batching, and safe soak count from logs.
  • Record Frostmourne room duration and the return threat/position state.
  • Document any custom health, armor, resistance, or damage tuning.
  • Resolve any mismatch between live item/spell tooltips and standard 3.3.5 records before updating assignments.
