How to participate↑ Top

The goal is simple: keep increasing the number and variety of useful AH listings. Players can help in whichever way fits.

Join

Bring a bank or auction alt

Keep the main in its current guild. Zalania handles invitations to The Merchant’s Guild.

Post

List useful items personally

Use the working Auctioneer setup and the current pricing guides to choose what to post and where to start pricing it.

Donate

Pass along useful extras

Materials, gear, recipes, and other useful items can be sent by in-game mail or handed over in an arranged trade with Zalania.

Ask

Get general help

Valdora can answer general questions and helps support the project, but is not a member of The Merchant’s Guild.

! Donations are currently unpaid. There is no reimbursement system for vendor value or vendor value plus 10%. Do not promise payment unless a real system is introduced later.

The website is also posted in the Hellscream Discord channel #garrosh-player-guides.

Privacy stays separate↑ Top

Helping the AH should not expose someone’s characters or guild ties. Public posts use general wording such as “community contributors” and “items donated to the guild.”

  • Mains and alts are not publicly connected.
  • Donations are not publicly attributed to individual players.
  • AH listings are not tied publicly to contributors.
  • Main-guild membership remains separate.
  • Information is recorded internally only when genuinely necessary.
  • No public roster connects bank alts or auction alts to mains.

Posting guidance↑ Top

Leave breathing room

Do not flood chat. Leave time between posts and avoid repeating identical wording back to back.

Rotate the message

Use different short versions so the reminder feels useful instead of automated.

Move details to whispers

Answer individual questions privately when a long public exchange would crowd the channel.

Protect contributors

Never reveal a contributor’s main, alt connection, main guild, donation, or AH listing.

Be clear about payment

Say that donations are unpaid. Do not promise vendor-value reimbursement or any future payment.

Keep claims modest

The AH has improved, but do not call it fully healthy, self-sustaining, or an official server program.
