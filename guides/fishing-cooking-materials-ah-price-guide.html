WotLK 3.3.5 Fishing + Cooking Materials AH Price Guide

Low-pop AH pricing sheet for Hellscream / Garrosh. Covers raw fish, cooking meats, eggs, clams, pearls, old-world leveling mats, quest/convenience cooking drops, rare catches, rare fishing poles/devices, vendor supplies, plus a few finished prices for major raid foods, feasts, and utility drinks.

Read first: Use Target Buyout as the normal price. Post small stacks. Use Quick for fast sales and High only when supply is scarce. AH durations are 4× normal: 12h ≈ 2 days, 48h ≈ 8 days.
Fishing/Cooking rule: Fish Feast fish, Northern Spices, Dragonfin Angelfish, and rare fishing gear can move very differently from ordinary leveling mats. Always check whether the item is actually tradeable on this server before pricing rare poles, hats, lines, or daily-bag rewards.
Expanded coverage note: Old-world meats and fish are tiny markets, but they matter on low-pop AHs because one missing ingredient can block a cooking leveler or quester. Most low-demand rows should be posted in small exact stacks, not dumped in bulk.

Small-stack mats

The lowest-demand old-world mats are grouped in Low-demand / should probably vendor. Post 2, 4, 5, 10, or 20 stacks based on recipe counts and current AH supply.

The should-probably-vendor rows are optional. If saving time matters, vendor those items instead of relisting every tiny old-world mat.
