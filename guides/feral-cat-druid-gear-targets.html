Emblems and tier↑ Top

Heroism and Triumph

  • Prioritize Mirror of Truth and a major weapon or armor gap.
  • Use Triumph catch-up pieces and T9 to replace leveling gear.
  • Do not hoard every emblem while the current set cannot support the rotation.

Tier progression

  • Build T9 as a bridge, then compare Cat T10 bonuses against actual offset pieces.
  • Keep hit and expertise visible while changing multiple slots.
  • Set bonuses do not replace uptime and Energy discipline.

Frost

  • Buy the largest reliable tier or off-set upgrade first.
  • Herkuml War Token is a later sustained-melee option, not the automatic first purchase.
  • Keep alternate hit, expertise, and trinket pieces.
