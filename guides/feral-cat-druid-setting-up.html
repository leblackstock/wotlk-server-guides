0/55/16 Fresh-80 self-contained Cat baseline

Use the fully allocated Cat build for Savage Roar, Rip, Shred, Energy, mobility, and solo utility. Treat bearweaving and encounter-specific glyph swaps as later options.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Cat engine

  • Mangle unlocks the core bleed-support attack.
  • Savage Roar turns combo points into a sustained physical-damage buff.
  • Berserk creates a planned low-cost Energy window.

Resource control

  • Omen of Clarity creates free-action opportunities.
  • Primal Precision supplies 10 expertise and refunds Energy from missed finishers.
  • King of the Jungle improves Tiger's Fury and supports planned burst.

Self-contained utility

  • Naturalist and Master Shapeshifter support the complete dungeon baseline.
  • Later raid flex points must preserve 71 allocated points and state what reliability is being traded.
  • This Cat page does not silently substitute the separate Bear tank baseline.

Form boundaries↑ Top

Stay Cat for damage

  • Use Cat Form for the normal damage priority.
  • Plan every utility cast that removes form, then return deliberately.
  • Do not shift repeatedly without a reason; form changes consume mana and globals.

Bear is an emergency role

  • Enter Dire Bear Form only for a named survival, pickup, or recovery job.
  • Announce the transition because threat behavior changes.
  • A Cat talent and gear set is not the dedicated Bear baseline.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Safe Cat opener

Enter Cat Form when needed, then use the selected hostile ability normally.

#showtooltip Cat Form /cast [nostance:3] Cat Form

Focus Maim

Stun a hostile focus without losing the damage target; fall back to the current target.

#showtooltip Maim /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Maim

Mouseover Rebirth

Leave form and combat-resurrect a dead friendly mouseover or target.

#showtooltip Rebirth /cancelform /cast [@mouseover,help,dead][help,dead] Rebirth

Emergency Bear

Enter the survival form directly; defensive abilities remain separate decisions.

#showtooltip Dire Bear Form /cast Dire Bear Form
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for movement and target-life planning.
  • A 3.3.5-compatible Cat timer that separates Savage Roar, Rip, Rake, and the Mangle debuff.
  • An Energy and combo-point display placed near the target and mechanic timers.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Savage Roar, Omen of Clarity, Tiger's Fury, and Berserk.
  • Details/Recount to review target uptime, misses, bleed uptime, deaths, and damage to assigned targets.
  • A threat display for Berserk, Swipe, and emergency Bear transitions.
