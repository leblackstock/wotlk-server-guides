HoTs

Pre-HoT predictable damage with Rejuvenation and Regrowth while avoiding wasteful blanket healing in small groups.

—
Lifebloom

Choose deliberately between rolling a stack, letting it bloom, and using Clearcasting for efficient healing and mana return.

—
Raid healing

Use Wild Growth for real clustered damage and Swiftmend for urgent recovery while preserving assignment coverage.

—
Mana and procs

Track mana, Clearcasting, Innervate, Revitalize, and expensive direct heals so recovery tools remain available.

—
Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard original-client WotLK 3.3.5a behavior is the baseline. Verify Lifebloom refunds, Wild Growth targeting, Swiftmend glyph behavior, Rejuvenation haste, Revitalize, and Tranquility coverage on Hellscream.

Before the pull

  • Apply Mark of the Wild or Gift of the Wild and confirm the tank has Thorns when appropriate.
  • Confirm tank, raid, cleanse, Innervate, Rebirth, and emergency-cooldown assignments.
  • Place the tank, focus, raid frames, mana, Clearcasting, and boss timers where all are visible together.
  • Carry reagents, water, Flask of the Frost Wyrm, food, and mana potions appropriate to the run.

First fresh-80 moves

  • Train every level-80 rank and install the complete 11/0/60 self-contained Restoration baseline and glyph set.
  • Enter normal and heroic dungeons immediately; practice pre-HoTs, Lifebloom decisions, Swiftmend, cleansing, and mana pacing before raid optimization.
  • Replace leveling gear with spell power, useful haste, Spirit or MP5, and enough Intellect to finish pulls safely.
  • Treat 735 haste with Celestial Focus or 856 without it as later raid goals under stated raid buffs—not as Fresh-80 dungeon gates.
