WotLK 3.3.5 Raiding with your Marksmanship Hunter

Raid scope: these are Marksmanship Hunter responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of Endless Rage for attack power.
  • Blackened Dragonfin for Agility or Hearty Rhino for Armor Penetration only when the set's model supports it.
  • Potion of Speed immediately before combat and again in the best legal burst window.
  • Saronite Razorheads or the server's best valid ammunition, pet food, and a repaired ranged weapon.

Confirm before pull

  • Misdirection target and whether the pull or a later add wave receives it.
  • Tranquilizing Shot and Silencing Shot assignments.
  • Trap location, crowd-control target, and kiting route.
  • Rapid Fire, Readiness, Call of the Wild, and potion timing.
  • Pet target rules and any phase where the pet must be recalled.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / control

Use Tranquilizing Shot on assigned enrages, spread for Legion Flame, and keep the pet out of avoidable hazards. On Anub'arak, help control burrowers with Frost Trap when assigned and use Volley only when stacked adds—not a priority target—justify it.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicBoss / spikes

Maintain boss damage, switch instantly to Bone Spikes, and use instant shots while relocating in Bone Storm. Recall or reposition the pet if its path would cross Coldflame; do not channel Volley into a single urgent spike.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAdds / interrupt

Misdirect add waves to the assigned tank, follow physical-versus-magic add priority, and preserve Silencing Shot for the assigned cast. Stay spread and move early from Death and Decay rather than finishing Steady Shot.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicBoss / beasts

Use Frost Trap or an assigned slow route before Blood Beasts reach the ranged group, switch immediately, and never let the pet or hunter be struck. Misdirection can stabilize beast pickup only if that is the raid's plan.

Festergut and Rotface

10 / 25Normal / HeroicBoss / movement

Stutter-step to spores, slime, and infection drop points while protecting Auto Shot. Use Deterrence only as a confirmed survival tool; it does not replace the required spore, movement, or raid positioning.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAdds / utility

Misdirect and burst Volatile Ooze or Gas Cloud by priority, move before Malleable Goo, and trap only where placement will not endanger the raid. Baseline Explosive Trap is dropped at the hunter's feet—there is no assumed ranged launcher.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicSwap / bite

On Princes, damage only the empowered target and handle Kinetic Bombs if assigned without sending the pet across unsafe paths. On Blood-Queen, follow bite order, move early for Pact and Swarming Shadows, and plan cooldowns around the air phase.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAdds / interrupt

Prioritize Blazing Skeletons and Suppressors, interrupt Frostbolt Volley when assigned, and Misdirect dangerous spawns. Volley is useful on stable stacked packs but never outranks a Skeleton threatening Lay Waste.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicBoss / tombs

Stop shots before Instability becomes lethal, control pet uptime around air phases, and break only assigned Ice Tombs. In phase three, use tomb line-of-sight to reset Mystic Buffet and do not let uncontrolled pet attacks finish a tomb early.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAll rolesNeeds Hellscream test

Misdirect Shambling Horrors or transition adds as assigned, Tranquilizing Shot Enrage, burn Val'kyr, and control Raging Spirits. A hunter may kite or soak Vile Spirits with an encounter-specific plan, but Deterrence, target behavior, and server scripting must be tested on Hellscream first.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicRealm damage

Stay in the assigned realm, move early with Fiery Combustion or Soul Consumption, and recall the pet before dangerous pathing. In twilight, plant between cutter rotations so Auto Shot and Steady Shot resume without drifting into the next beam.
