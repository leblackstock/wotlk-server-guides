    Pricing notes & legendRead before posting
    Read first: Every listed item is audited as epic quality, Bind on Equip, required level 80, and drop-sourced in the pinned 3.3.5 data. Post rare gear one copy at a time.
    Guide focus: Dropped BoE gear only. Crafted equipment, recipes, token purchases, reputation rewards, and BoP raid loot stay outside this guide.
    Pricing status: Use Target for the first listing, Quick when you want a faster sale, and High / Scarce only when the item is genuinely scarce and you are willing to wait. Post one copy at a time and record whether it sells.
