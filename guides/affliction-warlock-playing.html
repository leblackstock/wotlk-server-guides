Affliction Playing Guide

Manage DoTs, Haunt, Shadow Bolt, Drain Soul, movement, target life, curses, and utility as one priority system.

Core decision engine↑ Top

DoTs

Maintain effects only when the target will live long enough for their full value.

Haunt

Keep Haunt active and use Shadow Bolt to establish and refresh the supporting debuffs.

Execute

Below 25%, channel Drain Soul and refresh required effects immediately after a tick.

Resources

Maintain Fel Armor, plan Life Tap safely, and arrive with shards, pet, Healthstone, and Soulstone ready.

Default priority: Fel Armor and the Glyph of Life Tap buff → assigned curse → Shadow Bolt to establish Shadow Embrace → Haunt → Unstable Affliction → Corruption → Shadow Bolt filler above 25% → Drain Soul filler below 25%.

Opener

  • Use a safe pre-pull Life Tap when Glyph of Life Tap is equipped; enter combat with Fel Armor and the Felhunter ready.
  • Precast Shadow Bolt when the pull timer and tank allow it.
  • Apply Haunt, Unstable Affliction, the assigned curse, and Corruption after the opening debuffs are established.
  • Do not delay the whole opener waiting for a raid buff or proc that may never arrive.

Track this

  • Haunt, Unstable Affliction, Corruption, the assigned curse, and Shadow Embrace.
  • Glyph of Life Tap buff, mana, health, threat, and Soulshatter.
  • Target health at 35% and 25%, plus enough remaining life for each new DoT.
  • Felhunter target, health, range, and Spell Lock availability.

Durable target

Build the full engine

Default

Decision

Use the complete priority when the target will live long enough for every maintenance global to pay back.

Shadow Bolt›Haunt›Unstable Affliction›Corruption
  • Establish Shadow Embrace before evaluating the opening Corruption.
  • Refresh Haunt early enough that travel time does not create a debuff gap.

Failure to avoid: recasting every DoT early and replacing damaging ticks with maintenance globals.

Target below 25%

Drain Soul execute

Execute

Decision

Replace Shadow Bolt filler with Drain Soul while required effects stay active.

Haunt›Unstable Affliction›Drain Soul
  • Break the channel immediately after a tick when a required effect must be refreshed.
  • Restart Drain Soul after movement or a refresh; never wait for a full 15-second channel.

Failure to avoid: clipping before ticks or letting Haunt and Unstable Affliction disappear during the strongest phase.

Second durable target

Multi-DoT with a reason

Cleave

Decision

Add effects only when the target will survive long enough and the primary assignment remains safe.

Corruption›Unstable Affliction›Haunt
  • Corruption is the cheapest durable extension; add longer setup only when life expectancy supports it.
  • Haunt cannot reliably cover many targets; prioritize the assigned target.

Failure to avoid: blanketing short-lived adds and losing boss Haunt, execute ticks, or the required target swap.

Short-lived add

Skip slow setup

Swap

Decision

Use direct damage or a minimal DoT package when the add will die before a full ramp pays back.

Shadow Bolt›Haunt›Drain Soul
  • Use Drain Soul on an eligible dying target when it supports the assignment and shard supply.
  • Follow the raid's kill target instead of padding durable side targets.

Failure to avoid: finishing a five-global setup after the add is already dead.

Forced movement

Move on useful globals

Mobile

Decision

Refresh only effects genuinely due, tap safely, command the pet, or reposition through Demonic Circle.

Life Tap›Corruption›Demonic Circle: Teleport
  • Place Demonic Circle before the mechanic, not after movement has already begun.
  • Do not Life Tap into lethal incoming damage.

Failure to avoid: manufacturing early DoT refreshes every time movement begins.

Stacked pack

Seed stable targets

AoE

Decision

Use Seed of Corruption on a target likely to remain inside the pack until detonation.

Seed of Corruption›Rain of Fire›Shadowfury
  • Choose Rain of Fire when the pack will die before repeated Seeds detonate.
  • Respect crowd control and tank threat before opening with AoE.

Failure to avoid: seeding a target that leaves the stack or breaking assigned control.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Treating the priority as a fixed rotation and refreshing effects early.
  • Using long DoTs on targets that will die before their final ticks.
  • Missing Drain Soul ticks by clipping at arbitrary times.
Raid mistakes
  • Allowing Haunt to fall because travel time was ignored.
  • Life Tapping immediately before predictable lethal damage.
  • Losing the Felhunter or assigned Spell Lock to uncontrolled pet movement.
