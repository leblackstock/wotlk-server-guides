Demo Lock Raid DPS Guide

Handle Demonic Pact, Felguard uptime, Metamorphosis windows, add executes, movement, and encounter assignments.

Where this chapter fits: This chapter is later progression, not the starting expectation for a new level 80. Complete the self-contained setup, practice the priority and pet controls in normal and heroic dungeons, and enter raids only after the character and player are ready.
Raid scope: these are Demonology Warlock responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of the Frost Wyrm for planned raid progression; it is not required for ordinary dungeon entry.
  • Firecracker Salmon or Fish Feast for a practical spell-power food option.
  • Potion of Speed for a planned haste window; Potion of Wild Magic is a critical-strike/spell-power alternative whose timing depends on the specialization.
  • Soul Shards, a Healthstone, a Soulstone, repaired gear, and the correct pet before the pull.

Confirm before pull

  • Which curse or debuff the Warlock is responsible for and who covers it after a death or target swap.
  • Primary target, add priority, crowd-control assignment, and whether the pet stays on the boss or swaps.
  • Demonic Circle location and the movement mechanic it is intended to solve.
  • Metamorphosis windows, safe proximity opportunities, and Demonic Pact expectations.
  • Soulstone target, Healthstone availability, and Soulshatter timing.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utilityNeeds Hellscream test

Maintain the assigned curse and pet uptime across target changes. On Anub'arak, use low-health adds for Decimation only when the raid's damage plan permits, and verify Hellscream's proc behavior before relying on a specific execute-sniping route.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep boss effects running, switch to Bone Spikes immediately, and preserve the Felguard through Coldflame and Bone Storm. Use Metamorphosis at range if melee access is unsafe.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Follow add priority, keep the Felguard on the called target, and avoid uncontrolled Cleave around crowd control. Save a Metamorphosis window for a wave only when it does not sacrifice continuous boss or priority-add access.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Keep the Felguard on the boss unless the raid explicitly assigns pet swaps. Use direct damage on Blood Beasts, exploit Decimation only without compromising control, and never approach for Immolation Aura.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Switch hard to Volatile Ooze and Gas Cloud, keep the pet out of slime and bad paths, and move before Malleable Goo. A safe full ranged Metamorphosis window is better than a short lethal proximity attempt.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

On Princes, damage only the empowered target and redirect the Felguard promptly. On Blood-Queen, follow bite order, preserve pet uptime through movement, and align Metamorphosis with a full ground phase rather than the air transition.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Prioritize Blazing Skeletons and Suppressors, use Seed of Corruption on stable stacks, and redirect the Felguard without cleaving controlled targets. Low-health adds can feed Decimation for the next priority target.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Manage Unchained Magic and Instability, recall the Felguard for unsafe air transitions, and break only assigned tombs. Use Metamorphosis at range if phase-three proximity cannot be maintained safely.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicAll rolesNeeds Hellscream test

Keep boss effects and the Felguard active, burn Val'kyr and Raging Spirits by priority, and use Decimation from eligible adds only within the raid plan. Verify Hellscream pet pathing, Demonic Pact overwrites, and Vile Spirit behavior before adopting a specialized proximity route.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicDamage / utility

Stay in the assigned realm, pre-place Demonic Circle for cutter movement, and recall the Felguard before dangerous realm or path transitions. Treat Immolation Aura as optional; ranged Metamorphosis damage remains useful.
