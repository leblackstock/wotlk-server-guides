◆ Emblem and tier priorities↑ Top

Triumph

  • Talisman of Resurgence is the first high-impact purchase if you lack strong trinkets.
  • Use 245-level tier or offset pieces to repair weak slots, prioritizing Intellect and haste.
  • Libram of Veracity is not a general upgrade over Libram of Renewal; reserve it for Valithria-style unlimited-mana throughput.

Frost

  • Build toward the Holy T10 two-piece bonus.
  • Choose tier slots that avoid replacing unusually strong haste/MP5 offset pieces.
  • Purified Lunar Dust is usable, but it normally does not displace Meteorite Crystal, Soul Preserver, Solace, or Talisman for their intended jobs.

Tier strategy

  • T10 two-piece strengthens Divine Illumination and is the first set-bonus goal.
  • Do not force four-piece if the individual pieces destroy a better stat layout.
  • Keep older powerful pieces until the complete replacement is actually stronger.
