Channel

Fit complete Mind Flay ticks between higher-priority events.

Mana & support

Use Shadowfiend, Dispersion, Replenishment, and Hymn of Hope deliberately.

Server behavior on Hellscream

Standard original-client WotLK 3.3.5a behavior is the baseline. Shadow Word: Pain snapshot details, channel timing, boss immunities, and custom encounter scripting should be verified on Hellscream when observed behavior differs.

Before the pull

  • Activate Shadowform and Inner Fire; confirm Fortitude, Spirit, and Shadow Protection assignments.
  • Confirm the boss target, required utility, and that the equipped boss set reaches its spell-hit target.
  • Confirm whether the raid needs Vampiric Embrace, Mass Dispel, Silence, or a Dispersion soak.
  • Pre-pot only when the encounter and pull timer make the potion window safe.

First fresh-80 moves

  • Reach the boss spell-hit target and install visible DoT timers.
  • Farm Abyssal Rune and another functional trinket before chasing expensive ICC pieces.
  • Practice a five-stack opener on a training dummy until Shadow Word: Pain is never applied early.
  • Run heroics and Trial of the Champion while buying Triumph upgrades for the weakest slots.
