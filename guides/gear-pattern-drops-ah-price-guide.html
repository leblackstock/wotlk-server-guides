    Pricing notes & legendRead before posting
    Read first: Use Target Buyout as the normal price. Recipes and patterns sell as single items, not stacks. AH durations are 4× normal: 12h ≈ 2 days, 48h ≈ 8 days.
    Guide focus: 90 tradeable profession recipe items. Eighty-five have pinned loot paths; five limited-vendor recipes are retained and explicitly labeled because their AH value is convenience and restock access, not drop scarcity. Trainer, unlimited ordinary-vendor, reputation, BoP, and research-only recipes remain outside this guide.
    Tradeability note: Every listed recipe must appear unbound and tradeable in WotLK-style data. Known BoP recipes remain excluded unless this server explicitly changes their binding.
