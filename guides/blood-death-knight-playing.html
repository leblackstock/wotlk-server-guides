Blood Death Knight • Level 80

WotLK 3.3.5 Playing your Blood DK

Control resources around incoming damage. Threat is a requirement; resource availability at the lethal moment is the craft.

Combat engine Priority Playbooks Cooldowns Utility Sources

Core combat engine

Model: prepare → maintain → absorb → recover. This is a priority with reservations, not a repeating rotation.
Prepare runes

A Frost + Unholy pair is one Death Strike. A Blood rune can become Rune Tap or Vampiric Blood. Plan those costs before spending.

Maintain diseases

Icy Touch applies Frost Fever; Plague Strike applies Blood Plague. Glyph of Disease lets Pestilence refresh both.

Reserve RP

Icebound Fortitude and Anti-Magic Shell each cost 20 RP. Treat that reserve as locked whenever a lethal window is approaching; only the surplus belongs to Rune Strike.

Keep a last resort

Pre-summon or safely cast Raise Dead so Death Pact is executable. Minion threat must never alter the encounter’s position.

Death Strike correction: in WotLK 3.3.5 it heals 5% of maximum health for each of your diseases on the target. With two diseases and 2/2 Improved Death Strike, the nominal heal is 15% maximum health before Vampiric Blood and other healing modifiers. It is not based on damage taken in the previous five seconds.

Single-target priority

Survival first

  1. Be in Frost Presence and face the boss away from the raid.
  2. Keep the 20% attack-speed reduction from Improved Icy Touch unless another assigned DK owns it.
  3. Spend both native Blood runes often enough to keep Blade Barrier active.
  4. Preserve two diseases and a Frost/Unholy pair for Death Strike.
  5. Use Rune Tap after real health is missing; it is off the global cooldown.

Threat after safety

  1. Icy Touch in Frost Presence is the primary snap and single-target threat button.
  2. Rune Strike replaces the next melee after a dodge or parry. Queue it from RP surplus.
  3. Death Strike deals useful threat while returning health.
  4. Use Blood Strike or Pestilence to spend Blood runes; choose Pestilence when diseases need refreshing.
  5. Death Coil is a low-priority RP dump. This build lacks Lichborne, so it cannot self-heal with Death Coil.

Safe pull sequence

Icy Touch› Plague Strike› Death Strike› Blood Strike› Pestilence

If the pull needs immediate threat, add extra Icy Touch casts with Blood Tap or Empower Rune Weapon only when that does not consume a planned survival resource. Do not open LK by exhausting every rune and the five-minute reset.

Scenario 1

Routine boss tanking

Baseline

Maintain

Frost Presence, two diseases, Improved Icy Touch, Blade Barrier, and a 20-RP wall reserve.

Act

IT / PS›DS›Blood spend

Reserve: one Frost/Unholy pair and the Blood rune needed by the next heal or cooldown. Avoid: low-priority Death Coil or extra Icy Touch when it breaks that reserve.

Recover / LK25H: Death Strike after a swing, then Rune Tap if real health is still missing; this is the baseline between Soul Reapers.

Failure to avoid: spending the entire rune bar because every button is lit.

Scenario 2

Snap threat or pickup

Threat

Maintain

Frost Presence and the RP/rune cost of the next survival event.

Act

Dark Command› Icy Touch›Rune Strike

Reserve: one Frost rune for Icy Touch and 20 RP if a wall is imminent. Avoid: Death Grip when forced movement would rotate a frontal or cross Defile.

Recover / LK25H: call a resist, use the second taunt, and re-establish threat with Icy Touch; a Soul Reaper handoff is complete only after taunt success.

Failure to avoid: confusing a taunt with lasting threat; follow it with Icy Touch.

Scenario 3

Multiple targets

AoE

Maintain

Your primary target’s diseases and frontal control.

Act

Death and Decay›IT / PS›Pestilence›Blood Boil

Reserve: all three rune types for Death and Decay only when pickup control outweighs the lost Death Strike. Avoid: spending the whole bar on harmless ghouls.

Recover / LK25H: tab Icy Touch/Rune Strike into the Horror or Spirit, stabilize its facing, then rebuild a Death Strike pair; this tree is intentionally weaker at repeated AoE than Morbidity.

Failure to avoid: backing through enemies; dodge and parry do not work from behind.

Scenario 4

Movement and transitions

Position

Maintain

Boss facing, melee contact, and an escape path clear of Defile or raid cones.

Act

Chains of Ice›Death Grip›Icy Touch

Reserve: one Frost rune and a movement cooldown if the assignment requires it. Avoid: backpedaling through the boss or exposing the back during Quake.

Recover / LK25H: restore facing and melee range before threat dumping; Nitro Boosts are powerful, but failure behavior remains a Hellscream-specific test.

Failure to avoid: gaining a few yards while exposing your back to a boss swing.

Scenario 5

Planned burst

Cooldown

Maintain

20 RP, the required Blood rune, and a named healer/external.

Act

Personal CD›Taunt swap›Death Strike

Reserve: the exact cost of IBF/AMS or Vampiric Blood plus a recovery pair. Avoid: stacking every personal and external on one event.

Recover / LK25H: use IBF before impact, Vampiric Blood early enough for healer throughput, then Death Strike/Rune Tap after the Reaper tick or hasted swings.

Failure to avoid: stacking IBF, Vampiric Blood, trinkets, and an external on one Reaper, then having nothing for the next.

Scenario 6

Emergency recovery

Recover

Maintain

Enough awareness to call the emergency and stop threat dumping.

Act

Vampiric Blood›Rune Tap› Death Strike›Death Pact

Reserve: any runnable heal first; Empower Rune Weapon is the five-minute rebuild when the rune bar is empty. Avoid: continuing Rune Strike or Death Coil during recovery.

Recover / LK25H: add a Healthstone or potion in parallel, announce the gap, and preserve the ghoul until Death Pact is required. Do not enter the next Reaper without a written replacement layer.

Failure to avoid: dying with Rune Tap, a ghoul, Healthstone, or ERW still available.

Utility and unavailable tools

Available

  • Mind Freeze: melee interrupt; free in this exact Frost subspec if Endless Winter is selected by the tree.
  • Strangulate: ranged silence with a long cooldown; use for assigned caster control.
  • Chains of Ice and Death Grip: pickups and repositioning.
  • Horn of Winter: Strength/Agility buff and a no-target GCD when rebuilding RP.
  • Empower Rune Weapon: resets all runes and grants 25 RP; emergency recovery or a planned threat reset.

Not in 43/23/5

  • Lichborne: no Death Coil self-healing.
  • Anti-Magic Zone: not available.
  • Bone Shield: not available.
  • Mark of Blood: not selected by this exact tree; adding it means removing a point from the locked baseline.
  • Dancing Rune Weapon: deliberately skipped; it is a damage cooldown, not a defensive.
  • Improved Icy Talons: the 25-player raid supplies the 20% melee-haste buff.
Army rule: channeling Army of the Dead reduces damage taken based on dodge plus parry, but the ghouls taunt. On LK25H, use it only in a pre-approved raid plan where movement and target control are safe. Never press it ad hoc during Defile, a transition, or an add handoff.
