WotLK 3.3.5 Engineering Materials AH Price Guide

Low-pop AH pricing sheet for Hellscream / Garrosh. Focused on Engineering parts, raw inputs, explosives, ammo, powders, mount parts, and practical catch-up materials.

Read first: Use Target Buyout as the normal price. Post small stacks. Use Quick for fast sales and High only when supply is scarce. AH durations are 4× normal: 12h ≈ 2 days, 48h ≈ 8 days.
Practical overlap rule: Raw bars and stones are included only when Engineers commonly buy them directly or convert them into Engineering parts.
