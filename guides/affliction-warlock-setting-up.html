55/0/16 Fresh-80 self-contained Affliction baseline

Use the complete Suppression baseline while early gear is short on hit. Treat threat, range, and raid-composition swaps as later options.

Tree not loading? Open the complete build in the Wowhead calculator.

Affliction engine

  • Everlasting Affliction lets Shadow Bolt, Haunt, and Drain Soul refresh Corruption.
  • Pandemic enables critical Corruption and Unstable Affliction ticks.
  • Death's Embrace and Drain Soul define the execute transition.

Self-contained hit

  • Three points in Suppression provide 3% spell hit for all schools in Wrath.
  • The baseline therefore needs 14% from gear for a level-83 target before counting real group effects.
  • Do not remove Suppression until the replacement set and group assumptions are explicit.

Legitimate flex

  • Threat, range, and pushback talents can be adjusted for a known encounter or stable raid environment.
  • Any later swap must preserve 71 allocated points and state the new hit requirement.
  • Advanced Corruption snapshot tools belong after the basic priority is reliable.

Felhunter control↑ Top

Default state

  • Use the Felhunter for sustained damage and Spell Lock utility.
  • Bind pet attack, follow, and passive so target swaps and hazards do not decide for you.
  • Keep Spell Lock available manually when assigned to interrupt.

When the pet is unsafe

  • Recall before transitions and ground hazards.
  • Resummon only when the cast or Soul Shard cost will not fail the current mechanic.
  • Report pet death when it removes an interrupt or meaningful damage.

Practical 3.3.5a macros↑ Top

Focus Spell Lock

Interrupt the focus without changing the damage target; fall back to the current hostile target.

#showtooltip Spell Lock /cast [@focus,harm,nodead][] Spell Lock

Controlled pet attack

Start the filler and send the Felhunter to the selected target.

#showtooltip Shadow Bolt /petattack [harm,nodead] /cast Shadow Bolt

Stop and follow

Recall the pet immediately from a hazardous target or path.

/petpassive /petfollow

Demonic Circle return

Return to the pre-placed circle without a modern cursor command.

#showtooltip Demonic Circle: Teleport /cast Demonic Circle: Teleport
Client rule: these use original 3.3.5 macro conditionals and exact English spell names. Modern retail-only commands and cursor casting are intentionally excluded.

Addons and interface↑ Top

Essential

  • Deadly Boss Mods for movement, phase, and execute timing.
  • A 3.3.5-compatible DoT timer that separates each target and shows Haunt travel or expiration clearly.
  • A cast/channel bar that exposes Drain Soul ticks and latency.

Recommended

  • WeakAuras/TellMeWhen for Shadow Embrace, Glyph of Life Tap, execute health, and pet state.
  • Details/Recount to review target damage, DoT uptime, execute damage, misses, threat, and pet uptime.
  • A threat display that makes Soulshatter timing visible before aggro is lost.
