Balance Druid Playing Guide

Manage Eclipse, DoTs, cooldowns, movement, mana, target life, crowd control, and Druid utility as one decision system.

Core decision engine↑ Top

Lunar

Trigger Lunar Eclipse with Wrath, then protect the Starfire window from movement and needless refreshes.

Solar

Trigger Solar Eclipse with Starfire, then cast Wrath while using Starfall only where its radius is safe.

DoTs

Maintain Moonfire and Insect Swarm only when the target will live long enough for their useful ticks.

Mana and movement

Plan mana recovery, movement globals, positioning, utility, and safe returns to the Eclipse engine.

Default priority: Moonkin Form and group buffs → required Faerie Fire → useful Moonfire and Insect Swarm → safe Force of Nature and Starfall → Wrath until Lunar Eclipse → Starfire until Solar Eclipse → repeat.

Opener

  • Precast Wrath only when the pull timer, travel time, and tank allow it.
  • Apply required Faerie Fire, then Moonfire and Insect Swarm when the target will live for useful ticks.
  • Use Force of Nature where the treants can connect and survive; use Starfall only after checking its entire radius.
  • Continue Wrath until Lunar Eclipse procs, then spend the window with Starfire.

Track this

  • Eclipse: which direction can proc, which buff is active, and how much of the window remains.
  • Moonfire, Insect Swarm, Faerie Fire, and target life expectancy.
  • Starfall, Force of Nature, mana, threat, and Innervate.
  • Movement timers, Nature's Grace, and whether the next cast can finish before displacement.

No Eclipse active

Trigger the next window

Default

Decision

Use the spell capable of triggering the available Eclipse while preserving required effects.

Wrath›Eclipse›Starfire
  • Wrath criticals can trigger Lunar; Starfire criticals can trigger Solar.
  • Do not alternate every cast—the proc state determines the filler.

Failure to avoid: casting the empowered school while trying to trigger its own locked Eclipse direction.

DoT decision

Refresh for useful ticks

Maintain

Decision

Apply or refresh only when the target will live long enough and the global will not waste a critical Eclipse window.

Moonfire›Insect Swarm›Starfire
  • DoTs support sustained targets, movement, and debuffs.
  • Avoid refreshing both automatically inside every Eclipse.

Failure to avoid: turning a strong Eclipse window into repeated early maintenance globals.

Stacked enemies

Use controlled area damage

AoE

Decision

Choose Starfall, Hurricane, or direct focus damage according to pack safety, life, and movement.

Starfall›Hurricane›Typhoon
  • Audit the full Starfall radius before casting.
  • Use Typhoon knockback only when it helps the tank or saves a player.

Failure to avoid: pulling inactive enemies, breaking control, or scattering a positioned pack.

Forced movement

Move on useful globals

Mobile

Decision

Use genuinely due instant effects, utility, or planned movement rather than inventing early refreshes.

Moonfire›Insect Swarm›Typhoon
  • Start moving before the hazard reaches you.
  • Return to the Eclipse filler immediately after reaching safety.

Failure to avoid: standing still to finish a low-value cast or refreshing every effect far too early.

Mana pressure

Recover before empty

Mana

Decision

Use Innervate early enough to gain its full value and continue the priority without a dry phase.

Innervate›Moonkin Form›Wrath
  • Plan the first use from fight length instead of waiting for zero mana.
  • A requested healer Innervate is an assignment decision, not an automatic default.

Failure to avoid: saving Innervate until the remaining fight is shorter than its useful return.

Control or recovery

Use the Druid toolkit

Utility

Decision

Interrupt the damage priority when an assigned control, cleanse, mana, or resurrection action prevents a wipe.

Rebirth›Remove Curse›Cyclone
  • Confirm the resurrection target and safe location before casting.
  • Coordinate control so immunity and diminishing returns do not waste the global.

Failure to avoid: protecting personal damage while the assigned utility action is missed.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Treating Eclipse as a two-spell alternating rotation instead of a proc state.
  • Refreshing Moonfire and Insect Swarm automatically during every empowered window.
  • Casting Starfall before checking nearby packs and controlled targets.
Raid mistakes
  • Finishing a low-value cast while a movement mechanic reaches the character.
  • Waiting until empty mana to plan Innervate.
  • Ignoring an assigned cleanse, control, or Rebirth to preserve the damage meter.
