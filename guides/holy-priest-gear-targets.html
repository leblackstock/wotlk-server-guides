Emblems and tier↑ Top

Triumph

  • Buy Talisman of Resurgence when both trinkets are weak.
  • Use T9 pieces to repair the weakest armor slots and establish useful set bonuses.
  • Do not buy around a final BiS list while wearing quest greens elsewhere.

Frost

  • Build toward useful T10 healing bonuses.
  • Purified Lunar Dust is usable, but compare it against strong mana trinkets before spending.
  • Buy the largest immediate upgrade rather than banking emblems indefinitely.

Tier logic

  • Evaluate set bonuses and the actual replacement piece together.
  • Keep haste-heavy offset pieces when forcing tier would weaken the whole set.
  • Save old mana pieces for long progression fights.
