WotLK 3.3.5 Raiding with your Holy Paladin

Prepare your consumables, understand the damage pattern, and treat Beacon, Aura Mastery, Hands, and mana windows as encounter tools.

◆ Assignment rules↑ Top

Two active tanks

  • Beacon one tank and directly heal the other.
  • Reverse the pairing when one tank becomes the clear danger target.
  • Refresh Beacon before movement or a swap, not after it expires during the spike.

One active tank

  • Beacon the tank when you must assist the raid.
  • Direct-heal the tank when Beacon transfer would be mostly overheal.
  • Do not manufacture split healing when the encounter does not need it.

Special targets

  • Marks, debuffs, encounter NPCs, and realm-separated tanks may replace the normal pairing.
  • State the plan before the pull.
  • Use role names instead of vague “MT/OT” labels.
Using Divine Plea during raids
  • Use it during low incoming damage, transitions, forced movement, or while another healer covers the tank.
  • Pair it with Avenging Wrath or a throughput trinket when healing cannot pause.
  • Cancel it if the reduced healing becomes dangerous. Mana saved during a death is economically fascinating and strategically useless.

Movement healing

Blood Prince Council
Keleseth-Tank Healer
Keep Beacon on the physical tank and directly heal the Keleseth tank when assigned. Large health swings can follow poor orb coverage; use a real cast, not wishful Flash of Light confetti.

Blood-Queen Lana'thel
Tank + raid pressure
Beacon the off-tank or linked tank and direct-heal the active tank while Holy Light splash assists the melee group.
Air phase

Valithria Dreamwalker
Portal Healer
Enter assigned portals, maintain Emerald Vigor stacks, and spend the resulting throughput directly on Valithria. Refresh Beacon and Sacred Shield only when they support the plan rather than stealing a high-stack healing global.

Sindragosa
Tank healer

Trial of the Crusader

Northrend Beasts
Impale tanks
Beacon the non-active tank and directly heal the Impale tank. Refresh before a swap and save Hand of Sacrifice for a planned high-stack window.
Movement / debuffs
Keep instant casts ready for fire, poison, and Icehowl movement. Cleanse only the debuffs assigned to you.
Lord Jaraxxus
Incinerate Flesh
Immediately direct healing into Incinerate Flesh when assigned; the absorb must be satisfied before it erupts. Beacon remains on the tank unless the raid plan says otherwise.
Faction Champions
Utility fight
Cleanse, Freedom, Protection, and Salvation often matter more than a perfect healing parse. Stay mobile and use line of sight rather than attempting to out-heal every enemy cooldown.
Twin Val'kyr
Raid burst
Beacon the tank and direct Holy Light into grouped damage. Coordinate Aura Mastery with the raid's planned resistance aura and shield timing.
Anub'arak
Boss + Add Tank
Beacon one tank and directly heal the other while adds are active. In phase three, keep tanks safe without flooding the raid with unnecessary healing that increases Leeching Swarm returns.
Penetrating Cold
Know your Penetrating Cold targets before the pull. Use fast, controlled heals on assigned targets while tank healing remains covered.
