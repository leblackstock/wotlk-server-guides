Feral Bear Raid Tank Guide

Handle boss positioning, swaps, adds, cooldowns, survival sets, utility assignments, and encounters by mode.

Where this chapter fits: This chapter is later progression, not the starting expectation for a new level 80. Complete the self-contained setup, practice the role in normal and heroic dungeons, and enter raids only after the character and player are ready.
Raid scope: these are Feral Bear Druid responsibilities, not full encounter guides. Raid-leader assignments override a generic playbook.

Consumables and pull preparation↑ Top

Consumables

  • Flask of Stoneblood for the default health flask.
  • Fish Feast or equivalent Stamina food after verifying the available feast.
  • Indestructible Potion for a planned physical-damage window.

Confirm before pull

  • Confirm main tank, add tank, taunt, interrupt, external-cooldown, and battle-resurrection assignments.
  • Identify frontal direction, movement route, line-of-sight position, and the first survival window.
  • State whether baseline or enhanced attack-power reduction is actually present.

Trial of the Crusader

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignmentsNeeds Hellscream test

Face bosses away, plan taunts and cooldowns before each phase, and separate physical from magical survival needs. On Anub'arak, follow the assigned boss or burrower role and verify Hellscream add positioning and taunt behavior.

Lord Marrowgar

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Keep Marrowgar faced away, call Saber Lash coverage, and reposition cleanly after Bone Storm. Pick up or control Bone Spikes only under the raid's tank plan.

Lady Deathwhisper

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Gather assigned adds without dragging cleaves through the raid, interrupt eligible casts, and prepare the phase-two boss pickup before the shield breaks.

Deathbringer Saurfang

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Hold the boss stable, execute tank swaps on Rune of Blood, and never use uncontrolled movement or area threat that disrupts Blood Beast control.

Professor Putricide

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Follow the assigned boss or Abomination role exactly. Move the boss ahead of slime and Malleable Goo, communicate mutation energy or swap changes, and plan cooldowns for phase three.

Blood Prince Council and Blood-Queen Lana'thel

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

On Princes, control only the assigned active target and preserve orb or add paths. On Blood-Queen, face safely, coordinate the opening link, and plan cooldowns around known tank damage.

Valithria Dreamwalker

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Pick up assigned Abominations, Zombies, and other adds quickly, keep frontals away from healers, and use Bash or charge interrupts only on eligible priority casts.

Sindragosa

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Manage Frost Breath cooldowns and Mystic Buffet resets, position tombs according to the plan, and never turn the frontal or tail through the raid during phase-three movement.

The Lich King

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignmentsNeeds Hellscream test

Execute Soul Reaper cooldowns and taunt swaps, position Raging Spirits and Shamblings safely, and communicate every movement before Defile. Verify Hellscream-specific taunt and add behavior before relying on a narrow sequence.

Halion

10 / 25Normal / HeroicTank assignments

Stay in the assigned realm, face Halion away from the raid, move smoothly around cutters, and plan cooldowns for breaths and overlap. Do not allow realm movement to rotate the frontal through players.
