Feral Cat Playing Guide

Manage Energy, combo points, Savage Roar, bleeds, positioning, movement, target life, and utility as one priority system.

Combat enginePlaybooksCooldownsMistakesSources

Core decision engine↑ Top

Forms

Stay in Cat Form for the damage job and change form only for a deliberate movement, survival, or utility reason.

Resources

Pool Energy, build combo points, and spend only when Savage Roar and Rip remain safe.

Bleeds

Maintain Rake and a strong five-combo-point Rip only on targets that will live long enough.

Survival

Use Barkskin, Survival Instincts, movement, off-healing, and emergency Bear Form without abandoning the assigned DPS job.

Default priority: Cat Form and group buffs → Faerie Fire (Feral) when assigned → required Mangle (Cat) debuff → useful Savage Roar → Rake → Rip at five combo points → Shred builder from behind → Ferocious Bite only with safe timer room.

Opener

  • Use Feral Charge - Cat only when the path is safe and its movement value is needed.
  • Apply Faerie Fire (Feral) and Mangle (Cat) when those effects are assigned and not already covered.
  • Build a short Savage Roar, apply Rake, then build toward a five-point Rip.
  • Use Tiger's Fury at low Energy without wasting its return; begin pooling before the next finisher collision.

Track this

  • Savage Roar, Rip, Rake, and the required Mangle (Cat) debuff.
  • Energy, combo points, Tiger's Fury, Berserk, and Omen of Clarity.
  • Rear access for Shred, target life, travel time, and the raid's next forced movement.
  • Barkskin, Survival Instincts, Rebirth, and emergency-form expectations.

Durable target

Build the maintenance engine

Default

Decision

Keep Roar, Rip, Rake, and required bleed support useful while spending Energy through deliberate builders.

Savage Roar›Rake›Rip›Shred
  • Pool before refresh collisions so the required finisher lands on time.
  • Use five combo points for Rip whenever the target can live through it.

Failure to avoid: spending Energy immediately and then reaching a Roar or Rip deadline empty.

Timers collide

Pool before the deadline

Resource

Decision

Stop unnecessary builders, preserve combo points, and enter the refresh window with enough Energy to act.

Tiger's Fury›Savage Roar›Rip
  • Do not use Tiger's Fury near maximum Energy.
  • Roar uptime normally takes priority over an optional Bite.

Failure to avoid: overcapping early, then starving exactly when two finishers need attention.

No rear access

Stay active from the front

Position

Decision

Use Mangle and assigned utility until safe rear access returns instead of circling through danger.

Mangle (Cat)›Rake›Savage Roar
  • Do not expose the raid to cleaves merely to Shred.
  • Return behind the target only through the planned safe side.

Failure to avoid: losing several globals or dying while chasing a positional attack.

Urgent target

Match setup to target life

Swap

Decision

Use immediate builders and only the maintenance effects that can pay back before the target dies.

Feral Charge - Cat›Mangle (Cat)›Ferocious Bite
  • A short-lived add rarely deserves a full Rip setup.
  • Follow the assigned kill target rather than preserving boss uptime at the raid's expense.

Failure to avoid: finishing a five-point bleed after the urgent target is already dead.

Stacked pack

Swipe after threat

Adds

Decision

Use Swipe on a stable pack after the tank controls it, while preserving enough Energy for the next priority target.

Tiger's Fury›Swipe (Cat)›Mangle (Cat)
  • Wait for threat and respect crowd control.
  • Return to priority damage immediately when the pack or assignment changes.

Failure to avoid: opening with Swipe, pulling threat, or spending all Energy before an urgent target appears.

Danger or utility

Lose damage to save the pull

Survival

Decision

Use mitigation, form changes, Rebirth, or Innervate when that action prevents a death or fulfills an assignment.

Barkskin›Survival Instincts›Rebirth
  • Barkskin before predictable damage; Survival Instincts is temporary health, not immunity.
  • Announce an emergency Bear transition because it changes threat and damage output.

Failure to avoid: remaining in the damage priority while a known survival or recovery action is missed.

Mistakes that cost pulls↑ Top

Mechanical mistakes
  • Treating the priority as a memorized cast sequence instead of reading timers and Energy.
  • Using Ferocious Bite when Savage Roar or Rip cannot survive the Energy recovery.
  • Chasing rear access through cleaves, hazards, or excessive travel.
Raid mistakes
  • Applying full bleeds to targets that will die before they pay back.
  • Opening Swipe before the tank establishes the pack.
  • Refusing to use Barkskin, Rebirth, Innervate, or an emergency form because it lowers the meter.
