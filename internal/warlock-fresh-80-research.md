# Fresh-80 Warlock Guide Research Matrix

Status: implementation source sheet
Audience: newly capped level-80 Warlocks on an original-client WotLK 3.3.5a baseline
Server boundary: standard Wrath behavior is documented as the baseline; uncertain Hellscream behavior remains labeled `Needs Hellscream test`.

## Shared audience contract

- Normal and heroic dungeons are the entry path. The level-83 raid-boss hit cap is a later progression target, not a gate for beginning level-80 group content.
- Against a level-83 target, the self-contained spell-hit target is 17%, or about 446 hit rating before talents and group effects. At level 80, 26.23 hit rating is approximately 1% spell hit.
- Each guide must state which hit reductions are actually present. Never assume Misery, Improved Faerie Fire, Heroic Presence, or a particular raid composition.
- Spell power is the first uncapped throughput stat. Haste generally follows spell power, while critical strike rating and Spirit are useful but lower-priority for a newly capped character.
- Fel Armor, an active demon, a stocked Soul Shard bag, a Healthstone, and a Soulstone are ordinary preparation. Flasks, pre-potting, premium gems, and final-tier gear are later or optional progression.
- Pets must be placed on deliberate controls. The player remains responsible for pet target, passive/follow behavior, and avoiding lethal encounter mechanics.

## Affliction Warlock

### Baseline

- Talent build: 55/0/16.
- Calculator: `warlock/2350002030023510253500331151--550000051`.
- Default pet: Felhunter for sustained PvE damage and utility.
- Major glyphs: Glyph of Life Tap, Glyph of Quick Decay, Glyph of Haunt.
- Minor glyphs: Glyph of Drain Soul first; Glyph of Unending Breath and Glyph of Souls are convenience choices.

### Decision engine

1. Maintain Fel Armor and the Glyph of Life Tap spell-power buff without spending unnecessary globals.
2. Establish Shadow Embrace with Shadow Bolt before judging the opening Corruption.
3. Maintain Haunt, Unstable Affliction, Corruption, and the assigned curse.
4. Use Shadow Bolt as filler above 25% health.
5. At 25% health, use Drain Soul as filler and clip only immediately after a channel tick when a required effect must be refreshed.
6. Multi-DoT only targets that will live long enough for the applications to pay back their global cooldowns.

### Fresh-80 teaching emphasis

- Explain ramp time, DoT duration, and target-life judgment before advanced Corruption rollover optimization.
- Treat weapon swapping and highly optimized snapshots as later progression, not as the starting expectation.
- Explain that the selected build includes Suppression; readers still confirm the character-sheet total and the exact target level rather than copying one rating number blindly.

## Demonology Warlock

### Baseline

- Talent build: 0/56/15.
- Calculator: `warlock/-003203301135202530135221351-55000005`.
- Default pet: Felguard.
- Major glyphs: Glyph of Life Tap, Glyph of Felguard, Glyph of Quick Decay. Glyph of Metamorphosis is an encounter-dependent later option.
- Minor glyphs: Glyph of Drain Soul first; other minor slots are convenience.

### Decision engine

1. Keep the Felguard alive, attacking the correct target, and using Demonic Empowerment deliberately.
2. Maintain Curse of Doom when it can complete, Corruption for Molten Core, and Immolate.
3. Use Shadow Bolt as the ordinary filler.
4. Use Incinerate during Molten Core when Decimation is not active.
5. Below 35%, Soul Fire with Decimation takes precedence over the ordinary fillers.
6. Use Metamorphosis only when its full window is safe and useful. Immolation Aura and Shadow Cleave require deliberate proximity; they never justify standing in lethal mechanics.

### Fresh-80 teaching emphasis

- Demonic Pact is generated by the Warlock and Felguard; the build does not require another class to function.
- Personal survival and reliable pet uptime come before trying to maximize a raid-wide Demonic Pact snapshot.
- Teach ranged Metamorphosis use first. Melee-range Immolation Aura optimization is a later, encounter-aware extension.

## Destruction Warlock

### Baseline

- Talent build: 0/19/52.
- Calculator: `warlock/-033100301134-05203205210231051035030351`.
- Default pet: Imp.
- Major glyphs: Glyph of Conflagrate, Glyph of Incinerate, Glyph of Life Tap. Glyph of Immolate is a later comparison option.
- Minor glyphs: Glyph of Drain Soul first; other minor slots are convenience.

### Decision engine

1. Keep the Imp attacking safely and queue Firebolt through a controlled macro when appropriate.
2. Maintain Immolate before attempting Conflagrate.
3. Use Conflagrate and Chaos Bolt when available, subject to target life and movement.
4. Use Incinerate as the ordinary filler.
5. Use Life Tap during planned low-cost movement or before mana becomes an emergency.
6. Use Shadowfury and Seed of Corruption for control and stacked targets; do not force AoE onto packs that will die before setup pays off.

### Fresh-80 teaching emphasis

- The complete 0/19/52 baseline is easy to operate and does not leave unspent flex points.
- Replenishment and Blood Pact are useful contributions, not assumptions that excuse poor personal setup.
- A short-cooldown priority is not a fixed cast sequence. The guide must teach what to do when Immolate is missing, cooldowns collide, or the target will die early.

## Shared gearing path

### Immediate and inexpensive

- Quest rewards, normal dungeons, heroic dungeons, reputation pieces, crafted cloth, inexpensive BoEs, and Emblems of Heroism come before raid drops.
- Early hit repair candidates include Mark of the War Prisoner, Ebonweave pieces, Ring of Northern Tears, and ordinary hit gems.
- Reliable weapon/off-hand goals include Flameheart Spell Scalpel and Ward of the Violet Citadel.
- Sundial of the Exiled is a practical early emblem trinket; Forge Ember and Mark of the War Prisoner are dungeon alternatives.

### Later progression

- Trial of the Champion and the Icecrown five-player dungeons provide catch-up alternatives such as Abyssal Rune and Nevermelting Ice Crystal.
- Tier 9 and Tier 10 set bonuses are progression systems, not the first shopping list.
- ICC and Ruby Sanctum trinkets such as Muradin's Spyglass, Dislodged Foreign Object, Phylactery of the Nameless Lich, and Charred Twilight Scale remain clearly separated later targets.

## Original-client macro boundaries

- Use exact English 3.3.5a spell names.
- Supported patterns include `#showtooltip`, `[@focus]`, `[@mouseover]`, `[harm,nodead]`, `[pet]`, `/petattack`, `/petfollow`, and `/cast`.
- Do not use Retail-only cursor casting, modern talent-loadout commands, or macros that claim to make combat decisions for the player.
- Pet ability macros must explain the tradeoff between manual control and automatic use.

## Hellscream verification queue

- Affliction: Corruption rollover behavior, Drain Soul tick timing, and any custom execute or DoT-refresh behavior.
- Demonology: Demonic Pact overwrite behavior, Felguard pathing, pet inheritance, Metamorphosis cooldown handling, and Immolation Aura range.
- Destruction: Imp Firebolt queueing, Empowered Imp behavior, Shadowfury control, and Conflagrate/Immolate interaction.
- Shared: Soulstone rules, Demonic Circle behavior, pet avoidance/pathing, encounter-specific target immunities, and any custom item sources.
