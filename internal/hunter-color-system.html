Beast Mastery and Survival implementation boundary

Beast Mastery is identity-ready

Tawny bronze is approved for selected navigation and Beast Mastery-only emphasis. The future guide must decide whether pet command, burst windows, and owner-pet synchronization need separate mechanic lanes.

Survival is identity-ready

Ember orange is approved for selected navigation and Survival-only emphasis. The future guide must define recurring trap, Lock and Load, Explosive Shot, and movement lanes instead of coloring individual abilities ad hoc.

No placeholder mechanics: reuse class, spec, status, and existing named mechanic roles first. Add a new lane only when the guide teaches that concept repeatedly.

Semantic boundaries

Hunter green is not Sting

Class framing uses the Hunter token. Sting uses its more olive named mechanic token.

Hunter green is not success

Confirmed positive state retains the global success token and explicit wording.

Beast Mastery is not warning

Tawny bronze remains earthier and darker than warning amber, with labels carrying spec identity.

Marksmanship is not information

Precision steel uses named specialization tokens rather than global information cyan.

Survival is not danger

Ember orange never replaces the global danger role or lethal-error wording.

Pet does not always mean Beast Mastery

Pet mechanics may appear in every Hunter spec and must retain their own labels and context.
