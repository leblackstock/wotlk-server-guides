Regression boundaries

Color is never the only signal

Use labels, icons, position, borders, or wording alongside color.

Class color does not mean danger

DK crimson and Retribution red remain distinct from the danger role.

Spec blue does not mean information

Protection and Frost use named specialization tokens rather than the global info token.

Green requires a semantic check

Unholy identity, Addon compatibility, and generic success cannot share one unlabeled role.

Rarity remains canonical

Link destination and guide theme never alter item-quality colors.

Print removes ambience

Black-and-white output keeps labels and hierarchy without relying on tinted surfaces.
