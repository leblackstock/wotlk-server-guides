Warlock class identity

Warlock fel-violet

Class titles, shared Warlock framing, class-wide resources and utility, pet relationships shared by multiple specs, and broad Warlock links.

--class-warlock-accent: #8788ee
Class boundary

Fel-violet does not identify a selected specialization, an individual spell school, a pet assignment, warning, item rarity, or socket data.

Rule: every Warlock page remains recognizably Warlock before its specialization and mechanic colors enter the page.

Palette audit

Class violet retained

The shared violet is recognizable, readable, and lighter and bluer than the canonical epic-item role.

Affliction violet approved

The brighter curse violet supports DoT state while labels and token ownership keep it separate from epic rarity.

Demonology green approved

Fel green identifies pet and demon specialization state without replacing success or Auction House semantics.

Destruction orange approved

Flame orange communicates the fire specialization while remaining distinct from warning amber, danger red, and legendary-item data.

Readability gate: every approved Warlock class, specialization, and mechanic accent reaches at least 5.5:1 contrast against the primary card surface. Labels, borders, position, and wording still carry the meaning.

Semantic boundaries

Affliction violet is not epic rarity

Spec and DoT state use named Warlock tokens. Epic items retain the canonical quality token and item context.

Demonology green is not success

Confirmed positive state retains the global success token and explicit wording.

Demonology green is not Auction House teal

Warlock identity never recolors market selection, pricing, or section navigation.

Destruction orange is not warning

Risk and provisional Hellscream behavior remain warning amber with clear caution text.

Destruction orange is not legendary rarity

Legendary items retain canonical orange inside game-data contexts.

Pet color follows the taught mechanic

Felhunter, Felguard, and Imp do not share one generic pet color when their guides teach different decisions.
