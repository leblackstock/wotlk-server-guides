Druid class identity

Druid wild orange

Class titles, shared Druid framing, shapeshifting and utility relationships used by multiple specs, and broad Druid links.

--class-druid-accent: #e58b43
Class boundary

Wild orange does not identify a selected specialization, legendary item, warning, damage event, role, form, or individual spell school.

Rule: every Druid page remains recognizably Druid before its specialization, role, and mechanic colors enter the page.

Palette audit

Class orange adjusted

The Druid identity retains the familiar orange family while using a softer copper value than legendary-item orange or Destruction flame.

Balance indigo approved

Astral indigo supports eclipse state without borrowing rare-item blue, information cyan, or epic-item violet.

Feral hide approved

The light neutral hide value separates Cat and Bear identity from the warmer class frame and remains distinct from leaf green under tested red-green simulations.

Restoration green approved

The darker leaf value stays readable while labels and structure keep healing identity separate from success, Hunter, fel green, and Auction House teal.

Readability gate: every approved Druid class, specialization, and mechanic accent reaches at least 5.5:1 contrast against the primary card surface. The three specialization identities remain separated by lightness and hue under the tested red-green simulations.

Moonfire and Insect Swarm uptime, refresh timing, target life, and multi-target maintenance.

Feral mechanic system

Forms

Cat and Bear context, role changes, shapeshifting decisions, and abilities whose meaning changes with form.

--mechanic-feral-forms
Resources

Energy, Rage, combo points, pooling, spending, and the timing cost of changing form.

--mechanic-feral-resources
Bleeds

Rake, Rip, Lacerate, bleed-dependent effects, refresh timing, and target continuity.

--mechanic-feral-bleeds
Survival

Savage Defense, Barkskin, Survival Instincts, mitigation, emergency form use, and tank stability.

--mechanic-feral-survival
Feral contract: weathered hide identifies the Feral tree across both roles. Gold, rose, and steel blue separate resource, bleed, and survival decisions; text always identifies Cat, Bear, DPS, or tank context.

HoTs

Rejuvenation and Regrowth coverage, refresh timing, duration, target priority, and layered healing.

Semantic boundaries

Druid orange is not legendary or Destruction

Class identity uses named Druid tokens. Legendary items retain canonical game-data orange, while Destruction uses named Warlock spec tokens.

Balance indigo is not rarity or information

Rare and epic items keep canonical quality tokens; neutral factual callouts keep global information cyan.

Feral hide is not warning

Risk and provisional behavior remain warning amber with explicit caution wording.

Restoration green is not success or Auction House teal

Confirmed positive state and market navigation retain their fixed global and section roles.

Gold mechanics are not warnings

Solar, Feral resource, and Lifebloom accents always retain mechanic labels and never carry caution by hue alone.

Cat and Bear are roles, not extra specs

Both use Feral identity; explicit form and role labels distinguish their controls, priorities, and survival guidance.
