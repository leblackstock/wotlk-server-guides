Priest class identity

Priest silver

Class titles, broad Priest framing, class-wide utility, shared healing or shadow relationships, and links that remain relevant across all three specializations.

--class-priest-accent: #e7e8ea
Class boundary

Silver does not identify a selected specialization, a healing lane, a DoT state, warning, information, item rarity, or socket data.

Rule: every Priest guide should remain recognizably Priest before its specialization color enters the page.

Palette audit

Priest silver retained

The near-white class color is readable, faithful to the class, and leaves room for all three specs to separate cleanly.

Retained
Shadow violet retained

The existing violet is distinct from epic-item purple, remains readable on dark surfaces, and already supports the live Shadow guide well.

Retained
Holy gold rebuilt

The previous gold sat too close to Holy Paladin and warning amber. The new ivory-gold is lighter, calmer, and more Priest-specific.

Replaced
Discipline added

Warding indigo suggests shields and prevention without becoming generic information blue or copying Protection Paladin.

New
Readability gate: every approved Priest spec accent exceeds a 5.5:1 contrast ratio against the primary card surface. Labels, borders, position, and wording still carry the meaning.

Discipline implementation boundary

Identity-ready

Warding indigo is approved for Discipline-selected navigation, filters, controls, focus state, and Discipline-only technical emphasis.

Discipline
Mechanic palette pending

Do not color every shield, heal, or cooldown separately. Define two to four repeated lanes only after the Discipline guide establishes its actual teaching model.

Boundary: Discipline indigo is not global information blue and not the Addon section accent.

Semantic boundaries

Silver is class, not Common quality

Priest framing uses class tokens. Item quality keeps canonical game-data colors.

Discipline is not information

Warding indigo uses named specialization tokens and explicit labels.

Holy is not warning

Ivory-gold remains paler and quieter than warning amber, and warnings always use explicit status styling.

Shadow is not epic rarity

Void violet never recolors item links or replaces the canonical epic color.

Mechanics remain named

Triage, Serendipity, Prayer, DoTs, Weaving, Channel, and Mana keep labels or icons.

Print keeps structure

Headings, labels, borders, and order survive when ambience and tinted backgrounds disappear.
