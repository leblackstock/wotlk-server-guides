Protection hierarchy

Pink frames the class

Titles, class-wide guidance, and general Paladin relationships stay pink.

Blue signals tank state

Active chapter, selected filters, Protection controls, and mitigation emphasis use steel blue.

Violet and cyan teach timing

The 9/6 pair is a mechanic language, not a replacement for the Protection identity.

Warnings stay amber

Risk and uncertain Hellscream interactions do not become Protection blue.

Danger stays red

Failure and lethal errors remain globally consistent.

Rarity stays canonical

Gear links retain Wrath item-quality colors regardless of guide theme.

9-6-9 specimen

9-second lane

Use violet wherever the guide groups Judgement, Holy Shield, or Consecration by cooldown timing.

6-second lane

Use cyan for Hammer of the Righteous and Shield of Righteousness timing.

Tank decision

Use Protection blue for the selected pull type, active raid filter, or tank-specific instruction.

Paladin context

Use pink when the note applies to the class rather than the tank specialization alone.

Protection contract: pink says “Paladin,” blue says “Protection,” and the violet/cyan pair says “where this ability belongs in the rotation.”

Holy hierarchy

Pink frames the class

Class identity and broad Paladin relationships stay pink.

Gold signals Holy state

Active chapters, selected filters, Holy controls, and technical emphasis use gold.

Mechanics retain names

Beacon, direct healing, maintenance, and mana never collapse into one generic healing color.

Status remains global

Warnings and danger use the same tokens as every other guide.

Text carries assignments

Paired targets and healing lanes include labels, positions, icons, or borders.

Game data remains canonical

Item quality and sockets do not inherit Holy gold.

Healing engine specimen

Maintain

Keep Beacon, Sacred Shield, and Judgement support operating.

Cast

Choose the safe direct heal for the current damage pattern.

Transfer

Use the paired target instead of duplicating casts.

Recover

Use planned low-pressure windows and preserve emergency reserves.

Holy contract: gold identifies Holy decisions. Lavender, cyan, yellow, and mint explain what the healing engine is doing inside that decision.
Warning: meaningful risk or a provisional Hellscream interaction.
Danger: likely death, wipe, or destructive healing mistake.
